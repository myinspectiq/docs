## What You'll Learn

In this tutorial, you'll learn how to:

- Access your Inspection Report Content settings.
- Customize your Summary Report Notice and Important Report Information.
- Review and update your Inspection Scope, Limitations, and Exclusions.
- Personalize your General Report Information.
- Configure the definitions and severity descriptions used in your reports.

## Step 1: Access Inspection Report Content

1. Sign in to your InspectIQ account.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Select **Business Settings**.
4. Open **Inspection Report Content**.

This section allows you to manage the standard wording and definitions used throughout your inspection reports.

## Step 2: Customize Your Report Notices

Review the **Summary Report Notice** and **Important Report Information** sections.

These settings help communicate important information to your customers, including how they should interpret and use their inspection reports.

Update the wording as needed to reflect your professional practices and reporting requirements.

## Step 3: Review Your Inspection Scope and Limitations

InspectIQ provides dedicated sections for:

- **Inspection Scope** — Describes the general scope of your inspections.
- **Inspection Limitations** — Communicates limitations that may apply during an inspection.
- **Inspection Exclusions** — Identifies items or conditions outside the scope of your inspection.
- **General Report Information** — Provides additional information relevant to your reports.

Review each section to ensure the information accurately reflects your services and applicable professional standards.

## Step 4: Customize Report Definitions

Review the report definitions and severity descriptions used to explain inspection findings.

These descriptions help your customers understand the terminology and relative importance of observations documented in their reports.

Ensure your definitions are clear, consistent, and aligned with your reporting practices.

<Note>
  Report content should accurately reflect your inspection services, applicable standards of practice, and professional obligations. Review any changes carefully before using them in customer reports.
</Note>

## Why Customize Your Report Content?

Customizing your inspection report content helps you:

- Maintain consistent reporting language.
- Clearly communicate the scope and limitations of your inspections.
- Provide customers with understandable report definitions.
- Ensure your reports reflect your business and professional practices.

<Tip>
  Review your report content periodically to ensure it remains accurate as your business practices, services, or applicable standards evolve.
</Tip>
