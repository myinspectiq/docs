Once your report is generated, InspectIQ gives you flexible options for getting it into your client's hands. You can share a secure online link, send an email with a PDF attachment, or fire off a quick SMS — all from within the work order. You can also track whether your client has opened the report, resend it at any time, and download a copy for your own records.

<Note>
  Clients can view their report on any device — phone, tablet, or desktop — directly in their browser. They don't need to download an app or create an InspectIQ account.
</Note>

## Deliver a Report to Your Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order">
    From the **Dashboard** or **Jobs** list, tap the work order whose report you want to deliver.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Report tab">
    Tap the **Report** tab inside the work order and confirm your report has been generated. If it hasn't, tap **Generate Report** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Deliver Report">
    Tap the **Deliver Report** button to open the delivery options panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your delivery method">
    Select one or more of the following delivery methods:
    - **Secure Online Link** — Sends the client a unique URL they can open in any browser.
    - **Email with PDF attachment** — Sends an email to the client's address with the report attached as a PDF.
    - **SMS with link** — Sends a text message to the client's mobile number containing the secure report link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a personal message (optional)">
    Type a short personal message to include with the delivery. This appears above the report link in the email or SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap **Send Report**. InspectIQ delivers the report immediately and logs the delivery time on the work order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Include a brief personal message when delivering the report — something like a thank-you and a note on next steps. It adds a personal touch and helps clients know what to do after reading the report.
</Tip>

## Track Whether Your Client Has Viewed the Report

InspectIQ shows you exactly when your client opens their report. On the **Report** tab of the work order, you'll see a **Delivery Status** section that displays:

- The date and time the report was delivered
- Whether the client has opened the report (**Viewed** or **Not yet viewed**)
- The number of times the report has been viewed

This is useful for following up — if a client says they haven't received the report, you can instantly confirm delivery status and resend if needed.

## Resend or Share the Report Link

If a client loses their link or you need to share the report with someone else (such as a real estate agent), you can resend or copy the link at any time:

- **Resend** — Tap **Resend Report** to send the same link again via email or SMS.
- **Copy Link** — Tap **Copy Link** to copy the secure report URL to your clipboard and share it however you like.

The link remains the same each time, so resending doesn't create a new version.

## Download a PDF Copy

To save a copy of the report for your own records:

1. Open the **Report** tab on the work order.
2. Tap **Download PDF**.
3. The PDF saves to your device and can be stored, printed, or uploaded wherever you need it.

It's a good practice to keep a copy of every report you deliver as part of your business records.

## Report Link Expiry

By default, secure report links remain active for **90 days** after delivery. After that, the link will no longer be accessible to the client. You can adjust the default expiry period in **Settings → Business Settings → Report Templates**.

If a client needs access after a link has expired, simply open the work order and tap **Resend Report** to generate a fresh active link.
