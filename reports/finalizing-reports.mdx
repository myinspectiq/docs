Once your inspection findings are reviewed, use InspectIQ to prepare your reports. Review the written content and both report documents before sending them to your client.

## Open the Report Builder

1. Select **Inspections** and open the inspection.
2. Confirm that the applicable area overviews and findings have been reviewed.
3. Select **Generate Report** to open the Report Builder.

## Generate and review the summary

The Report Builder includes the **Report Title**, **Executive Summary**, **Safety Concerns**, **Major Concerns**, and **Maintenance Recommendations** sections.

1. Select **Generate AI Summary** to prepare suggested content from your inspection findings.
2. Review each section for accuracy, relevance, and clarity.
3. Edit any wording that does not reflect your observations.
4. Select **Save Report** to preserve your changes.

<Warning>
  AI-generated summaries require inspector review. Confirm that the report reflects the conditions actually observed and does not introduce unsupported conclusions.
</Warning>

## Finalize your report

After reviewing the Report Builder content, save any edits and use **Finalize Report** and its on-screen prompts to prepare the report for the next stage. Then review the generated documents in the Reports preview.

<Note>
  Saving report content and finalizing the report are separate steps. The Report Builder's editing and finalization controls are described in the existing Help Centre documentation; they are not shown in the available Reports recording.
</Note>

## Regenerate after changes

If you change inspection findings, area overviews, photos, or other report content after documents have been generated, rebuild the PDFs before delivery.

1. Open the relevant report preview.
2. Select **Regenerate Reports**.
3. Confirm regeneration when prompted.
4. Review the updated **Summary Report** and **Full Report**.

<img
  src="/images/03-regeneration-confirmation.jpg"
  alt="03 Regeneration Confirmation"
  title="03 Regeneration Confirmation"
/>

Regeneration rebuilds both report versions using the current inspection information.

## Next: deliver the reports

Once you've reviewed both documents, return to the Reports page to download or send the appropriate versions to your client. See [Managing & Delivering Reports](/reports/managing-delivering-reports).
