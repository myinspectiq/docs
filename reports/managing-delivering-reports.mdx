Use **Reports** to review generated inspection documents, track delivery status, and send the correct report versions to your client.

## Find a Report

1. Select **Reports** in the main navigation.
2. Search by property, client, or inspector, or use the **Status**, **Payment Status**, and **Date Range** filters.
3. Select **Preview** on the applicable inspection.

The dashboard groups reports into **Draft**, **Ready to Send**, and **Sent**. You can also change the sort order and, when available, switch between **My Work** and **Team**.

## Regenerate Reports After Changes

If inspection information changes after the PDFs were generated:

1. Open **Preview**.
2. Select **Regenerate Reports**.
3. When regeneration completes, review both **Summary Report** and **Full Report** again.

The recording confirms that regeneration rebuilds both report versions, including their latest inspection content and cover photo.

  src="/images/04-regeneration-confirmation.jpg"

<Note>
  Regeneration updates the generated documents. It does not replace the inspector's responsibility to check their contents.
</Note>

## Download a Report

In **Preview**, choose the report version you want and select **Download PDF**. The Reports list also displays **Summary** and **Full** download actions for reports already marked as Sent.

## Check Payment Status

The Reports list shows payment states such as **Unpaid** and **N/A**. An unpaid report can display **Payment required before release** and a **Send Reminder** action.

Follow your business's payment and release requirements before sending.

## Send a Report

1. Open the relevant report's **Preview**.
2. Select **Send Report**.
3. Choose the **Summary Report**, **Full Inspection Report**, or both.
4. Confirm the recipient email address, and add any authorized **CC** recipients if needed.
5. Select **Send Report** to deliver the selected documents.

After sending, use the **Sent** status to find delivered reports.

<Warning>
  Reports contain client and property information. Verify the recipients and review the attached document versions before sending.
</Warning>

## Before You Deliver

- [ ] The report versions being sent reflect your latest inspection edits.
- [ ] The observations, recommendations, and photographs have been reviewed.
- [ ] Any required payment conditions have been addressed.
- [ ] The recipient addresses and selected attachments are correct.

For preparing and finalizing inspection documents, see [Generating & Finalizing Reports](/reports/finalizing-reports).
