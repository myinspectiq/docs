InspectIQ turns your approved inspection findings, observations, and photos into professional reports. Use the **Report Builder** to prepare and review your documents, then manage delivery from **Reports**.

## Access Your Reports

You can open reporting in two places:

- **From an inspection:** Go to **Inspections**, open the inspection, then select **Generate Report** to enter the Report Builder.
- **From the Reports page:** Select **Reports** in the main navigation to find, preview, and manage inspection reports.

<Tip>
  Review your inspection findings and area overviews before preparing your report. See [Completing an Inspection](/inspections/completing-inspection).
</Tip>

## Understand the Two Report Types

InspectIQ prepares two complementary documents.

### Summary Report

A concise view of important inspection findings, including safety and major concerns and relevant maintenance recommendations. It helps clients understand what deserves attention first.

### Full Inspection Report

A detailed record organized around the inspection, including approved findings, observations, supporting photographs, and recommendations.

<Note>
  The **Summary Report** accompanies the **Full Inspection Report**; it does not replace it. Clients should review both.
</Note>

## Meet the Report Builder

The Report Builder provides editable content for your report, including:

| Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Report Title | Identifies the report. |
| Executive Summary | Summarizes the overall inspection. |
| Safety Concerns | Highlights documented safety-related findings. |
| Major Concerns | Calls out significant concerns. |
| Maintenance Recommendations | Provides relevant maintenance guidance. |

Select **Generate AI Summary** to draft summary text from your inspection information. Review and edit that text before using it in a final document.

<Warning>
  AI-generated content is a suggestion. The inspector is responsible for verifying that the report accurately reflects the conditions observed and the approved findings.
</Warning>

## Preview, Finalize, and Deliver

Your reporting workflow is straightforward:

1. **Prepare** — Review the Report Builder content and save your changes.
2. **Preview** — Check both the Summary Report and Full Inspection Report.
3. **Finalize** — Finalize when the content is accurate and complete.
4. **Deliver** — Use **Reports** to manage and send the documents to your client.

If inspection details change after reports have been generated, **Regenerate Reports** to rebuild the documents, then preview them again.
