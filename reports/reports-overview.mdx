InspectIQ organizes your inspection reports in one place so you can review what your client will receive and manage the documents through delivery.

## Open Reports

Select **Reports** from the main navigation to open the Reports dashboard.

<img
  src="/images/01-reports-dashboard-1.jpg"
  alt="01 Reports Dashboard 1"
  title="01 Reports Dashboard 1"
/>

The dashboard shows your report records, including the property and client, inspection date, report type, inspector, status, payment information, and available actions.

You can search by property address, client, or inspector. Filters are available for report status, payment status, and date range, along with sorting. In a team account, **My Work** and **Team** help you switch the view.

## Understand Report Statuses

The dashboard groups reports into three stages:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Report still in progress. |
| **Ready to Send** | Finalized and ready for delivery. |
| **Sent** | Delivered to the client. |

The report list also displays payment information. Depending on the report, payment requirements may affect whether it can be released.

## Summary Report vs. Full Report

InspectIQ provides two report views. From **Preview**, switch between **Summary Report** and **Full Report** to inspect each PDF before delivery.

### Summary Report

The **Summary Report** gives clients a shorter view of the inspection, highlighting key concerns and important findings.

### Full Report

The **Full Report** contains the detailed inspection document, including findings and supporting information organized into report sections.

## From Inspection to Delivery

1. **Review your inspection** — Check the findings, supporting photos, and approved content.
2. **Prepare and finalize** — Work through report preparation and finalization before delivery.
3. **Preview both PDFs** — Use the Summary Report and Full Report tabs, PDF viewer, and **Report Sections** navigation to review the output.
4. **Manage delivery** — From the report preview, the available actions include **Send Report**, **Regenerate Reports**, and **Download PDF**.

<Tip>
  If inspection content changes after report generation, regenerate the reports and review the updated PDFs before sending them.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  InspectIQ assists with report preparation, but you are responsible for checking that the final documents accurately reflect the inspection.
</Warning>
