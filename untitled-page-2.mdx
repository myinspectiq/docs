Subscription & Billing lets you review your current InspectIQ plan, monitor report usage, purchase additional reports, and manage your billing information.

## Access Subscription & Billing

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Subscription & Billing** from the left-hand menu.

## Your Current Subscription

At the top of the page, you'll see an overview of your current subscription.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Whether your subscription is currently active. |
| **Plan** | Your current InspectIQ subscription plan. |
| **Renews / Ends** | The date your current subscription renews or ends. |
| **Reports This Period** | The number of reports used compared with your available allowance. |

### Monitor Report Usage

The **Reports This Period** indicator shows how many reports you've used during your current subscription period.

For example, **10 / 40** means you've used 10 of your 40 available reports.

Check this indicator to monitor your usage and determine whether you need additional reports.

## Change Your Subscription Plan

The **Change plan** section displays the available InspectIQ subscription plans.

Each plan shows its monthly price, included inspection allowance, and number of team seats.

Your active plan is marked **Current**.

### Switch Plans

1. Locate the plan you want to change to.
2. Review its included reports, seats, and monthly pricing.
3. Select **Switch** beside the plan.
4. Follow the prompts to complete your plan change.

<Note>
  Subscription plans have different report allowances and team seat limits. Review the available options before switching plans.
</Note>

## Purchase Additional Reports

If you need more reports during your current subscription period, you can purchase an additional report pack.

Available options shown in InspectIQ include:

- **+10 Reports**
- **+25 Reports**

These are one-time purchases that add reports to your current subscription period.

### Buy Additional Reports

1. Scroll to **Need more reports this month?**
2. Choose the report pack you want.
3. Select **Buy**.
4. Complete the payment through Stripe Checkout.

The additional reports are intended to supplement your existing subscription allowance for the current period.

<Tip>
  Check your remaining reports before purchasing an add-on pack. You may find that switching plans better suits your inspection volume.
</Tip>

## Manage Your Billing

InspectIQ uses Stripe to manage subscription billing and payments.

### Open the Billing Portal

1. Scroll to the bottom of **Subscription & Billing**.
2. Select **Manage billing (Stripe portal)**.
3. You'll be directed to Stripe's secure billing portal.

Use the portal to review and manage the billing options available for your subscription.

<Note>
  Billing information and available management options are handled through Stripe.
</Note>

## Review Your Subscription Regularly

We recommend reviewing your subscription when:

- Your monthly inspection volume increases.
- You need additional report capacity.
- You're adding inspectors to your team.
- Your billing information needs updating.
- You're considering changing subscription plans.

Keeping your subscription aligned with your business needs helps ensure you have the appropriate report allowance and team capacity.
