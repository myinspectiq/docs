Credentials & Standards lets you configure your professional identification, licensing information, association memberships, and Standards of Practice.

This information helps identify the inspector and the professional standards followed by your inspection business.

## Access Credentials & Standards

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Credentials & Standards** from the left-hand menu.

You'll see three sections: **Inspector / Owner Details**, **Credentials & Licenses**, and **Standards of Practice**.

## Inspector / Owner Details

This section contains the primary inspector or business owner's identification information.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner / Inspector Name** | The name of the inspector or business owner. |
| **Default Signature Name** | The name used to identify the inspector when signing inspection documents. |

Review both fields to ensure the correct professional name is displayed.

## Credentials & Licenses

Record your professional licensing and association information.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Company Licence Number** | Your company's applicable inspection licence or registration number. |
| **Association** | The professional home inspection association you belong to, if applicable. |
| **Association Number** | Your membership or certification number. |

### Update Your Credentials

1. Locate **Credentials & Licenses**.
2. Enter or update your company licence number, if applicable.
3. Enter your professional association.
4. Enter your association membership or certification number.

<Note>
  Licensing and certification requirements vary by province, state, and jurisdiction. Complete the fields that apply to your business.
</Note>

## Standards of Practice

This section identifies the professional Standards of Practice your inspections follow.

A Standard of Practice (SOP) establishes the general scope, procedures, and limitations applicable to an inspection.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **SOP Name** | The full name of the Standard of Practice. |
| **SOP Short Name** | An abbreviated name used to identify the standard. |
| **SOP Version / Date** | The version or publication date of the standard. |
| **SOP Link** | A website link to the applicable Standard of Practice. |

### Configure Your Standard of Practice

1. Locate **Standards of Practice**.
2. Enter the full **SOP Name**.
3. Enter the **SOP Short Name**.
4. Specify the applicable **SOP Version / Date**.
5. Enter the **SOP Link**, if available.

<Tip>
  Include a direct link to your applicable Standard of Practice so the correct reference is readily available when reviewing your inspection documentation.
</Tip>

## Before Your First Inspection

We recommend confirming that:

- Your inspector or owner name is correct.
- Your default signature name is accurate.
- Your company licence information is current, if applicable.
- Your professional association and membership details are correct.
- Your Standard of Practice reflects the requirements you follow.
- Your SOP version and reference link are up to date.

<Note>
  InspectIQ allows you to record professional credentials and standards, but it does not replace your responsibility to maintain the licences, certifications, or memberships required to perform inspections in your jurisdiction.
</Note>
