## Your First Inspection, Made Simple

New to InspectIQ? Follow these four steps to get up and running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Business">
    Configure your account, business profile, and inspection preferences.

    [Account Setup Guide](/getting-started/account-setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create & Schedule a Work Order">
    Enter your client's information, add property details, select inspection services, and complete scheduling and agreements.

    [Creating Work Orders](/work-orders/creating-work-orders)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture Your Inspection Findings">
    Take photos, record observations, and use InspectIQ's AI-assisted tools to help document your findings.

    [Capturing Findings](/inspections/capturing-findings)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Generate Your Report">
    Review your findings, make any necessary adjustments, and generate a professional inspection report.

    [Reports Guide](/reports/overview)
