## Get Up and Running Fast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Sign up at [app.inspectiq.com](https://app.inspectiq.com) and complete your profile, including your name, email, and password
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Business Settings">
    Configure your report templates, inspection categories, and payment preferences in [Business Settings](/settings/business-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Work Order">
    Add a client, schedule the inspection, and send the agreement for signing — all from the [Work Orders](/work-orders/overview) section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the Inspection & Generate Your Report">
    Capture photos, record findings, and let InspectIQ's AI help you write the report. Deliver it to your client in one tap.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **New to InspectIQ?** Start with the [Getting Started guide](/getting-started/overview) for a complete walkthrough of every feature.
</Tip>
