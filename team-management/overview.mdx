Team / Members lets Team Owners manage the inspectors associated with their InspectIQ business account.

You can invite new members, review existing team members, manage pending invitations, and monitor your available seats.

## Access Team / Members

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Team / Members** from the left-hand menu.

You'll see the **Invite Member** button, your current seat usage, existing members, and any pending invitations.

## Understanding Team Seats

Your subscription determines how many team seats are available.

The **Seats Used** indicator shows how many seats are currently occupied compared with your plan's allowance.

For example, **Seats used: 1 of 3** means one seat is occupied out of three available.

<Note>
  Your Team Owner account counts toward your total seat usage. Pending invitations do not reserve a seat until they are accepted.
</Note>

If your available seats are full, InspectIQ displays a message indicating that you need to remove a member or upgrade your subscription to invite additional inspectors.

## Invite a Team Member

To invite an inspector to join your team:

1. Open **Team / Members**.
2. Select **\+ Invite Member**.
3. Follow the invitation prompts to invite your team member.
4. Review the invitation under **Pending Invites**.

Invited inspectors must accept their invitation before becoming active team members.

## View Your Team Members

The **Members** section displays inspectors who have joined your team.

Use this section to review your existing team membership and available seats.

If no additional inspectors have joined, InspectIQ displays a message indicating that no members have joined yet.

## Manage Pending Invitations

The **Pending Invites** section displays invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted.

Each pending invitation shows:

- The invited email address.
- The date the invitation was sent.
- An option to cancel the invitation.

### Cancel a Pending Invitation

1. Locate the invitation under **Pending Invites**.
2. Select **Cancel** beside the invitation.
3. The invitation will be cancelled.

<Tip>
  Review pending invitations periodically to keep your team list organized and ensure invitations are going to the correct email addresses.
</Tip>

## When Your Team Is Full

If you've reached your subscription's seat limit, you'll need to free up an existing seat or upgrade to a plan that supports more members before adding another inspector.

The **Seats Used** indicator helps you monitor your team's available capacity.

## Before Inviting Inspectors

We recommend checking that:

- You have available seats under your subscription.
- You have the correct email address for each inspector.
- You have reviewed any outstanding invitations.
- Your business settings are configured for your team's inspection workflow.

<Note>
  Team management features are available to Team Owners. Access to these settings may vary depending on your account role and subscription.
</Note>
