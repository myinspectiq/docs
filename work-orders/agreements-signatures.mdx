InspectIQ manages inspection agreements within each Work Order. Once the appointment is scheduled, you can send the agreement to your client, follow its signing status, and countersign it to complete the process.

## Open the Agreement

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Open the scheduled Work Order.
3. Scroll to **Work Order Status** and the highlighted **Next Step** section.
4. Review the agreement status and available actions.

## Review and Send the Agreement

When the Work Order shows **Send the Agreement**, review the generated document before sending it.

1. Select **Preview Agreement** and check the client, property, pricing, and terms.
2. Return to the Work Order.
3. Select the available send-agreement action.
4. Confirm that InspectIQ reports the agreement was sent.

<Note>
  Agreement wording and templates are managed separately under **Settings → Business Settings → Templates & Agreements**.
</Note>

## Wait for the Client to Sign

After the agreement is emailed, the Work Order displays **Waiting for Client Signature**, and its agreement status shows **Sent to Client**.

![Agreement Awaiting Client](/images/agreement-awaiting-client.png "Agreement Awaiting Client")

While waiting, the following actions are available:

- **View Agreement** — Open the agreement.
- **Resend Agreement** — Email the agreement again.
- **Copy Sign Link** — Copy the link to the signing page.
- **Mark as Signed (Offline)** — Record an agreement signed outside the online signing workflow.

<Note>
  **Sent to Client** does not mean the agreement is signed. Check the status before moving to the next stage.
</Note>

## Client Signature

The client opens the signing link, reviews the agreement, accepts the signing terms, and completes the required signature fields.

After the client signs, InspectIQ updates the Work Order to the **Awaiting your countersignature** step.

## Inspector Countersignature

1. Return to the Work Order after the client has signed.
2. Open the countersignature action in **Next Step**.
3. Review the agreement and select **Agree & Start Signing**.
4. Complete the required inspector signature fields and finish the signing process.

![Inspector Countersignature](/images/inspector-countersignature.png "Inspector Countersignature")

<Warning>
  Review the agreement before signing. Do not countersign a document containing incorrect inspection details or terms.
</Warning>

## After Both Parties Sign

When the signing process is complete, the Work Order displays **Complete (Signed)** for the agreement and **Inspection Ready** as the next step.

InspectIQ automatically creates the linked inspection as a draft. Select **Open Inspection** to enter the inspection workspace.

![Agreement Complete Inspection Ready](/images/agreement-complete-inspection-ready.png "Agreement Complete Inspection Ready")

You can also use **View** or **Download** beside the completed agreement in **Work Order Status**.

<Note>
  The **Payment** status is tracked separately from agreement signatures. A signed agreement can appear alongside an unpaid balance.
</Note>

## Before Opening the Inspection

Confirm the agreement is **Complete (Signed)** and that the correct inspector, appointment, and property details are shown in the Work Order.

<Tip>
  If you need to change your standard agreement language, update the template in Business Settings before preparing future agreements.
</Tip>
