An inspection agreement is the contract between you and your client that sets out the scope of the inspection, your liability terms, and the fee. Having a signed agreement in place before you start work protects both you and your client and helps avoid disputes after the report is delivered. InspectIQ makes the whole process digital — you can attach a template, send the agreement for e-signature via email or SMS, and track whether your client has signed, all from within the work order.

<Note>
  E-signatures collected through InspectIQ are legally binding in Australia under the *Electronic Transactions Act 1999* and equivalent state legislation. Each signed agreement is timestamped and linked to the signatory's verified email address or phone number, creating a clear audit trail.
</Note>

## Attach an Agreement Template to a Work Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order">
    Navigate to the relevant work order from your dashboard. The **Agreements** section appears below the client and property details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an agreement template">
    Tap **Attach Agreement** and select a template from your saved templates list. If you haven't set up any templates yet, go to **Business Settings > Agreement Templates** to upload or create one. You can maintain separate templates for different inspection types — for example, a standard building inspection agreement and a separate combined building & pest template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and customise (optional)">
    InspectIQ pre-fills the agreement with your client's name, the property address, the inspection type, and the scheduled date from the work order. Review the populated agreement and make any one-off adjustments before sending if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the agreement for e-signature">
    Tap **Send for Signature** and choose your delivery method — **Email**, **SMS**, or **Both**. Your client receives a link that opens the agreement in their browser. They can read the full document and sign with a finger or stylus on mobile, or with a mouse on desktop. No app download is required on their end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the signature status">
    Return to the work order at any time to check the agreement status badge:

    - **Sent** — The link has been delivered but the client hasn't signed yet.
    - **Viewed** — The client has opened the agreement.
    - **Signed** — The client has completed the e-signature. The work order is ready to progress.

    If your client hasn't signed close to the inspection date, tap **Resend** to send them a reminder with a fresh link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View and download the signed agreement">
    Once the status shows **Signed**, tap **View Agreement** to open the completed document. Tap **Download PDF** to save a copy for your records. Signed agreements are also stored permanently against the work order in InspectIQ so you can retrieve them at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not begin an inspection until the client's agreement status shows **Signed**. Starting work without a signed agreement may compromise your legal protection if a dispute arises after the report is delivered. If a client is on-site and hasn't signed yet, you can send the SMS link and ask them to sign on the spot before you commence.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Save yourself a step on every job by setting a **default agreement template** in **Business Settings > Agreement Templates**. When a default is configured, InspectIQ automatically attaches it to every new work order, so you only need to tap **Send for Signature** rather than selecting the template each time.
</Tip>
