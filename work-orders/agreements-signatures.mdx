InspectIQ lets you generate and manage inspection agreements directly from your Work Orders.

Agreements use your configured templates and inspection information to prepare documents for client and inspector signatures.

## Access Inspection Agreements

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Open the Work Order you want to manage.
3. Locate the agreement section within the Work Order.

From here, you can generate an agreement, review its status, and complete the signing process.

## Generate an Agreement

Once the Work Order has been prepared and the inspection appointment is scheduled, you can generate the inspection agreement.

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Select **Generate Agreement**.
3. Review the available agreement details.
4. Generate the agreement using your configured template.

InspectIQ uses information from the Work Order and your agreement template to populate the document.

This may include:

- Company and inspector information
- Client information
- Property address
- Inspection details
- Inspection fees
- Agreement terms and conditions

<Note>
  Agreement content and default templates are configured under **Settings → Business Settings → Templates & Agreements**.
</Note>

## Preview Your Agreement

Before sending the agreement, review its contents.

1. Locate the generated agreement.
2. Select **Preview Agreement**, where available.
3. Review the document for accuracy.

Pay particular attention to the client name, property address, inspection fees, and agreement terms.

<Tip>
  Always review your agreement before sending it, especially when you've recently updated your agreement template or business settings.
</Tip>

## Send the Agreement to Your Client

Once the agreement is ready:

1. Open the agreement section in the Work Order.
2. Select the available option to send the agreement.
3. Review the recipient information.
4. Send the agreement to your client.

The client can then review and electronically sign the agreement.

<Note>
  Ensure the client's email address is correct in the Work Order before sending the agreement.
</Note>

## Client Signature

The client reviews the agreement and completes the electronic signature process.

InspectIQ tracks the agreement's progress within the Work Order.

You can return to the Work Order to check whether the client has completed their signature.

## Inspector Countersignature

After the client signs, the inspector can complete the required countersignature.

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Locate the agreement requiring your signature.
3. Review the agreement.
4. Complete the inspector signing step.

This completes the agreement's electronic signature process.

<Note>
  The available signing actions depend on the agreement's current status and whether the client has already signed.
</Note>

## Monitor Agreement Status

Use the Work Order to track the agreement throughout the signing process.

Depending on its progress, you may see options to:

- Review the agreement.
- Send the agreement to the client.
- Check signature progress.
- Complete the inspector countersignature.
- Access the completed agreement.

The available actions change as the agreement progresses.

## What Happens After Signing?

Once the required agreement steps are complete, the Work Order can proceed to the inspection stage.

Select **Open Inspection** from the Work Order to access the inspection workspace.

This is where you'll capture photos, document findings, and prepare inspection reports.

## Before Starting Your Inspection

We recommend confirming that:

- The inspection appointment is correct.
- The client information is accurate.
- The agreement reflects the correct inspection services and fees.
- The client has completed their signature.
- The inspector countersignature is complete, where required.

<Tip>
  Configure your agreement template before creating your first Work Order to make agreement preparation faster and more consistent.
</Tip>
