Work orders are the backbone of every job you run in InspectIQ. Each work order acts as a single job record that brings together everything you need: your client's contact details, the property address, the type of inspection being performed, the scheduled date and time, the signed agreement, and the current status of the job. Whether you're booking a new client or following up on a completed inspection, the work order keeps all the moving parts organised in one place.

## Work Order Lifecycle

Every work order in InspectIQ moves through a clear set of stages from the moment you create it to the moment the job is done.

- **Draft** — The work order has been created but is not yet scheduled. Use this stage to capture client and property details before committing to a date.
- **Scheduled** — A date, time, and (optionally) an assigned inspector have been confirmed. The client can receive an automated confirmation at this stage.
- **In Progress** — The inspection is underway. The inspector can access the report template and begin logging findings on-site.
- **Complete** — The inspection is finished, the report has been delivered, and the job is closed out.
