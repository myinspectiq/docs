After creating a Work Order, you can schedule the inspection in one of two ways: **send appointment options to your client** or **choose the date and time yourself**.

## Open Scheduling

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Open the Work Order you want to schedule.
3. Find the **Schedule the Inspection** section.
4. Choose **Send Time Options to Client** or **Choose Date & Time Myself**.

## Send Time Options to Client

Use this option when you want the client to select a suitable appointment from times you offer.

1. Select **Send Time Options to Client**.
2. In **Offer time options**, enter a start and end date and time for each option.
3. Select **\+ Add time option** if you need another appointment choice.
4. Review your choices and select **Send options**.

The scheduling window allows **3 to 6 time blocks**. Each block uses a one-hour end-time default; adjust the start and end times to suit the inspection.

![Offer Time Options](/images/offer-time-options.png "Offer Time Options")

### While You're Waiting for the Client

After you send the options, InspectIQ creates a booking request. The Work Order shows that the options were sent and that you're **Waiting for Client to Choose a Date**.

From the booking request, you can:

- **View Options** — Review the times you offered.
- **Resend link** — Send the booking link again.
- **Copy** — Copy the booking link.
- **Set Myself** — Schedule directly instead.
- **Cancel session** — Cancel the outstanding booking request.

![Booking Options Sent](/images/booking-options-sent.png "Booking Options Sent")

<Note>
  While a booking request is outstanding, the appointment has not yet been selected. Don't treat **Options Sent** as a confirmed inspection date.
</Note>

## Choose Date & Time Myself

Use this method if you've already confirmed an appointment with the client or want to set the schedule directly.

1. From **Schedule the Inspection**, select **Choose Date & Time Myself**.
2. Enter the **Set Inspection Date & Time** value.
3. Select **Schedule**.
4. Review the date and time shown under **Work Order Status**.

![Schedule Date Myself](/images/schedule-date-myself.png "Schedule Date Myself")

## Check Your Scheduling Status

The **Work Order Status** section shows the scheduling state:

| Scheduling state | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not Scheduled** | No inspection appointment has been set. |
| **Options Sent** | Appointment choices have been sent and the client has not yet selected one. |
| **Scheduled** | An inspection date and time are set. |

After scheduling directly, the Work Order displays the scheduled date and time and moves to the next step: preparing the inspection agreement.

## What Happens Next?

Once the inspection date and time have been set, continue in the Work Order to **Generate Agreement** or **Send Agreement**, depending on its current agreement status.

<Tip>
  Confirm the appointment duration and allow enough time for travel and any additional services before offering appointment options.
</Tip>
