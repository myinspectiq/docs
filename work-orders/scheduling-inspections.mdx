InspectIQ gives you two ways to schedule a home inspection: choose an appointment yourself or send available date and time options to your client.

Both methods are managed directly from the Work Order.

## Access Inspection Scheduling

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Open the Work Order you want to schedule.
3. Locate the scheduling options.

Choose the method that works best for your inspection.

## Send Date & Time Options to Your Client

Use this option when you want your client to choose from appointment times you provide.

### Offer Appointment Times

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Select **Send Date & Time Options**.
3. Add the dates and times you want to offer.
4. Review your proposed appointment options.
5. Send the options to your client.

Your client can review the available appointments and select their preferred option.

### Client Selects an Appointment

Once your client selects an available date and time, the Work Order can proceed with the confirmed appointment.

<Tip>
  Offer appointment times that account for travel between properties and the expected inspection duration.
</Tip>

## Choose Date & Time Myself

Use this option when you've already agreed on an inspection appointment or prefer to schedule it directly.

### Schedule the Appointment

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Select **Choose Date & Time Myself**.
3. Choose the inspection date.
4. Select the inspection time.
5. Review the appointment details.
6. Confirm the schedule.

The selected appointment is associated with the Work Order.

## Inspection Duration

InspectIQ uses the inspection duration configured in your Business Settings as a starting point.

The duration can be adjusted for individual Work Orders when necessary.

To review your default inspection duration, navigate to:

**Settings → Business Settings → Inspection Setup → Defaults**

<Note>
  Inspection duration is an estimate of the time required to conduct the inspection. Allow additional time when necessary for larger properties or additional services.
</Note>

## Review Your Scheduled Inspection

After scheduling, open the Work Order to review the appointment information.

Confirm that:

- The inspection date is correct.
- The inspection time is accurate.
- The expected duration is appropriate.
- The correct inspector is assigned, if applicable.
- The client's contact information is accurate.

Scheduled Work Orders appear in the **Scheduled** stage of the Work Orders dashboard.

## What Happens Next?

Once the inspection is scheduled, the next step is to prepare the inspection agreement.

From the Work Order, you can generate the agreement using your configured template and proceed with the required signatures.

<Tip>
  Review your agreement template under **Business Settings → Templates & Agreements** before sending agreements to clients.
</Tip>
