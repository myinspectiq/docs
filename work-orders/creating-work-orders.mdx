A Work Order brings together the property, inspection services, pricing, and contact information needed before scheduling an inspection.

## Start a New Work Order

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Select **\+ New Work Order**.
3. Complete the form, beginning with **Property Info**.

![New Work Order Property](/images/new-work-order-property.png "New Work Order Property")

## Property Info

Enter an **MLS #** or start typing a Canadian address in **OR Address**. Select an address suggestion when available, or select **Lookup** to retrieve available listing information using an MLS number.

Review any returned information before continuing. Lookup may not provide every field, and you can enter or correct details manually.

Complete or review:

- **Inspection Type** — Select the type of inspection.
- **Services Needed (Add-ons)** — Select any additional inspection services.
- **Year Built** and **Square Footage** — Enter the available property information.
- **Property Type** — Select the appropriate property type.

<Note>
  The services offered here come from **Business Settings → Fees & Services**.
</Note>

## Location & Pricing

Review the **Property Address**, **City**, **Province**, **Postal Code**, and **MLS Number**. Correct anything that is missing or inaccurate.

![Property Lookup And Pricing](/images/property-lookup-and-pricing.png "Property Lookup And Pricing")

### Fees and Taxes

The form uses your configured business pricing as a starting point. Review the **General Fee** and **Tax Rate** and adjust them for this Work Order if necessary.

### Discount Override

Under **Per-WO Discount Override**, choose **\$ Dollar** or **% Percentage** and enter an amount when you need a Work Order-specific discount.

### Fee Summary

Check the **Fee Summary** to confirm the general inspection charge, selected add-on services, applicable tax, and total.

![Fee Summary And Inspection Details](/images/fee-summary-and-inspection-details.png "Fee Summary And Inspection Details")

<Tip>
  Check selected add-on services and the final total with your client before proceeding.
</Tip>

## Inspection Details (Optional)

You can enter an **Inspection Date & Time**, **Duration (Hours)**, and **Notes** now, or leave the appointment date and time empty to confirm later through scheduling.

The duration is initially populated from your inspection defaults and can be adjusted for this Work Order.

## Client / Homebuyer

Enter the client's **Full Name**, **Email**, and **Phone**. Add a mailing address if applicable.

<Note>
  Verify the client's email carefully. InspectIQ uses it in later scheduling and agreement steps.
</Note>

## Buyer's Agent and Seller's Agent

If applicable, enter the agent's **Full Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, and **Brokerage** in the appropriate section. These sections are optional.

## Create the Work Order

1. Review the property and contact details.
2. Confirm services, fees, taxes, and any discount.
3. Select **Create Work Order**.
4. On a Team account, complete the **Assign Work Order** prompt if shown.

InspectIQ then opens the Work Order details, where you can continue with scheduling.

## What Happens Next?

From the Work Order details, choose either **Send Time Options to Client** or **Choose Date & Time Myself** to schedule the appointment. After scheduling, continue with the inspection agreement process.

<Note>
  Creating a Work Order does not mean the appointment has been confirmed or the agreement has been signed. Those steps are handled separately.
</Note>
