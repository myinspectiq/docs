**Inspection Preferences** controls how photos captured during inspections are saved on your device. This is a personal setting and does not change your team's preferences.

## Open Inspection Preferences

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **My Settings**.
3. Select **Inspection Preferences**.

  src="/images/01-inspection-preferences.jpg"

## Save Inspection Photos to Device

Use **Save Inspection Photos to Device** to control whether additional local copies of captured inspection photos are saved:

- **On:** Save copies to your device, where supported.
- **Off:** Do not save additional local copies through this preference.

When local saving is enabled, InspectIQ organizes photos in an **InspectIQ** collection, with an album for each inspection named using the property and inspection date.

<Note>
  This setting applies to the InspectIQ mobile apps, which are **Coming Soon**. It does not change the photos stored with your inspections in InspectIQ.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If you choose to save local copies, keep an eye on available device storage, especially when capturing many inspection photos.
</Tip>
