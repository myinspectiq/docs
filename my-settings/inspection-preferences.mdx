Inspection Preferences lets you customize how InspectIQ handles photos captured during inspections.

These preferences apply to your individual account, not your entire inspection team.

## Access Inspection Preferences

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **My Settings**.
3. Select **Inspection Preferences** from the left-hand menu.

## Save Inspection Photos to Device

The **Save Inspection Photos to Device** option allows InspectIQ to save local copies of photos taken during an inspection.

When enabled, photos are saved to a dedicated **InspectIQ** folder on your device, where supported.

This makes it easier to access your inspection photos outside the application.

### Enable or Disable Photo Saving

1. Locate **Save Inspection Photos to Device**.
2. Turn the toggle **ON** to save copies of inspection photos locally.
3. Turn the toggle **OFF** if you don't want additional copies saved to your device.

<Note>
  Your original inspection photos remain stored in InspectIQ regardless of this setting. Turning off local photo saving does not remove photos from your inspections.
</Note>

## When Should You Enable This?

Saving inspection photos to your device may be useful if you:

- Want personal backup copies of your inspection photos.
- Need to access photos outside InspectIQ.
- Prefer to maintain a local photo library organized by your device.

<Tip>
  If you enable local photo saving, periodically review your device's available storage space, particularly if you conduct multiple inspections each week.
</Tip>
