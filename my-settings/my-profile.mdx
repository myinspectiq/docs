Your **My Profile** page contains your personal information and account details. It is separate from the company-wide information under Business Settings.

## Open My Profile

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **My Settings**.
3. Select **My Profile**.

## Update Your Profile Photo

In **Profile Photo**, select **Change Photo** to upload a replacement, or **Remove Photo** to clear it.

The page recommends a square **400 × 400 px** JPG or PNG and indicates the image is resized before upload.

## Edit Your Personal Information

Under **Personal Information**, review or update your:

- **Full Name**
- **Phone**
- **Email**

Look for the green **Saved** indicator to confirm your changes have been stored.

## Understand Your Account Details

**Account Details** displays your account type, such as **Team Owner**. This is informational; the recording does not show an account-type editor on this page.

## Choose Whether You Perform Inspections

If your account includes the **Inspection Role** section, use **I perform inspections** to indicate whether you personally carry out inspections. The page shows your seat usage and explains that enabling this role counts as one seat.

<Note>
  The interface warns that turning off your inspection role can be blocked when you have in-progress work. Review the message on screen before changing it.
</Note>

For company information, licensing, and inspection setup, use [Business Settings](/settings/business-settings).
