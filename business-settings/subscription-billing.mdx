Manage your InspectIQ subscription and report usage under **Settings → Business Settings → Subscription & Billing**.

## Review your current subscription

The top of the page shows your subscription **Status**, **Plan**, **Renews / ends** date, and **Reports this period** usage. Check these details to understand your current plan and remaining reporting allowance.

## Change your plan

Under **Change plan**, your current plan is marked **Current**. Other available plans show a **Switch** button.

1. Review the available plans and their included report allowances.
2. Select **Switch** beside the plan you want to explore.
3. Review any confirmation or checkout details before completing a change.

<Note>
  Plan names, prices, and included report allowances may change. Use the values shown in your InspectIQ account when making a billing decision.
</Note>

## Buy additional reports

If you need more reports during your billing period, scroll to **Need more reports this month?**

1. Choose an available add-on pack, such as **+10 Reports** or **+25 Reports**.
2. Select **Buy**.
3. Review the payment details in the Stripe checkout and complete the purchase if you wish to proceed.

## Manage billing

Select **Manage billing (Stripe portal)** to access the external billing management portal. Follow the options available in that portal for your account.

<Tip>
  Before buying additional reports, check **Reports this period** and your current plan allowance at the top of this page.
</Tip>
