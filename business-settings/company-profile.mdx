Your **Company Profile** stores the business details InspectIQ uses across your inspections, reports, and agreements.

## Open Company Profile

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Company Profile**.

<img
  src="/images/01-company-profile.jpg"
  alt="01 Company Profile"
  title="01 Company Profile"
/>

## Add Your Company Logo

In **Company Logo**, use **Upload Logo** to add your branding, or **Change Logo** to replace an existing image. Select **Remove** if you need to clear it.

The interface accepts **PNG or JPG** images, resizes uploads to **512 px**, and preserves PNG transparency. Your logo is used on inspection reports and agreement PDFs.

## Enter Your Business Information

Review these fields under **Company Information**:

- **Company Name** — the name used on reports, agreements, and team invitations.
- **Legal Business Name** — your company's registered legal name.
- **Tax Registration Number** — enter your applicable Canadian tax registration number, if required for your business.
- **Default Currency** — confirm the currency used by your business (for example, **CAD**).

## Review Contact Details

Complete the **Contact Address** fields, including:

- Contact email and phone number
- Address line, city, province or state
- Postal or ZIP code and country

Under **Online Presence**, enter your business website.

<img
  src="/images/02-contact-and-website.jpg"
  alt="02 Contact And Website"
  title="02 Contact And Website"
/>

## Check Your Updates

After editing, watch for the **Saved** confirmation shown in the Company Profile interface. Revisit the fields to confirm the information is accurate before generating client-facing reports or agreements.

<Tip>
  Use the same official company name, contact details, and logo you want clients to see on your inspection documents.
</Tip>

For fees and tax rates, continue to **Fees & Services** under Business Settings.
