Company Profile is where you manage your business information in InspectIQ.

Your company name, logo, contact details, and other business information help identify your company throughout the platform, including on inspection reports and agreements.

## Access Company Profile

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Company Profile** from the left-hand menu.

## Company Logo

Upload your company logo to personalize your InspectIQ account and inspection documents.

### Upload or Change Your Logo

1. Locate the **Company Logo** section.
2. Select **Change Logo**.
3. Choose your company logo from your device.
4. Review the updated logo.

InspectIQ supports **PNG and JPG** images. Images are resized to 512 pixels before upload, and PNG transparency is preserved.

To remove an existing logo, select **Remove**.

<Tip>
  For best results, use a high-quality PNG logo with a transparent background.
</Tip>

## Company Information

This section contains your company's primary business details.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Company Name** | Your business name, used on reports, agreements, and team invitations. |
| **Legal Business Name** | Your company's registered legal name. |
| **Tax Registration Number** | Your business tax registration number, if applicable. |
| **Default Currency** | The currency configured for your business. |

Review these details to ensure they accurately reflect your business.

<Note>
  Your Company Name is shared across your InspectIQ account and is used in various business documents and communications.
</Note>

## Contact Address

Maintain your company's contact information and business address.

Available fields include:

- Contact Email
- Contact Phone
- Address Line
- City
- Province / State
- Postal / ZIP Code
- Country

Keep these details accurate so your business information remains consistent.

## Online Presence

The **Online Presence** section contains your company's website information.

Enter your business website address using its complete URL, including \`https://\`.

This helps maintain a consistent business profile within InspectIQ.

## Review Your Company Profile

Before creating your first Work Order, we recommend checking that:

- Your company logo is uploaded and displays correctly.
- Your Company Name and Legal Business Name are accurate.
- Your tax registration information is correct.
- Your default currency is appropriate.
- Your contact information and address are current.
- Your website information is accurate.

<Tip>
  Review your Company Profile whenever your business information changes to keep your inspection documents and company details up to date.
</Tip>
