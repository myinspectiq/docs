Use **Templates & Agreements** to manage the wording that goes into your inspection agreements. Before sending agreements to clients, review the language to ensure it suits your business and applicable requirements.

Go to **Settings → Business Settings → Templates & Agreements**.

<img
  src="/images/01-templates-agreements.jpg"
  alt="01 Templates Agreements"
  title="01 Templates Agreements"
/>

## Edit your agreement template

Select **Open Template Editor** to open the agreement template library, then choose a template to edit. The editor includes a document body and available merge fields for inserting inspection and client details.

1. Open the template library and select the agreement you want to edit.
2. Review and edit the agreement wording.
3. Use the available merge fields where information should be populated from the Work Order.
4. Save the template using the editor's **Save Template** control.

<img
  src="/images/03-agreement-template-editor.jpg"
  alt="03 Agreement Template Editor"
  title="03 Agreement Template Editor"
/>

Select **Preview Agreement** from Templates & Agreements to inspect the document's layout and wording before using it for a client.

## Customize agreement content

Expand **Agreement Content** to edit the standard inspection language that can be inserted into the agreement. The recording shows fields for:

- **Inspection Scope** — what the inspection includes.
- **Inspection Limitations** — limitations of the inspection and its observations.
- **Inspection Exclusions** — services or conditions outside the inspection scope.

These fields are associated with agreement merge tokens. Adjust the text carefully so it reflects the services you actually provide.

## Review company policies

Expand **Policies** to edit the agreement's standard policy language, including cancellation, rescheduling, confidentiality, and third-party reliance terms.

<img
  src="/images/02-agreement-policies.jpg"

  title="02 Agreement Policies"
/>

<Note>
  The page displays a saved-state indicator for these settings. Check that your changes have been saved before leaving.
</Note>

## Use the agreement in a Work Order

When preparing a Work Order agreement, InspectIQ uses the configured agreement template and applicable Work Order details. Preview the generated agreement before sending it for signatures.

For the full workflow, see [Work Orders Overview](/work-orders/work-orders-overview).

<Warning>
  Default agreement wording is a starting point, not a legal-compliance guarantee. Have your agreement reviewed for your jurisdiction, standards of practice, insurer requirements, and business policies before using it with clients.
</Warning>
