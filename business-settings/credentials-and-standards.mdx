Use **Credentials & Standards** to keep your professional information and inspection standards up to date. InspectIQ uses these details on reports and agreements.

## Open Credentials & Standards

1. Select **Settings** in the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Credentials & Standards**.

  src="/images/01-credentials-and-standards.png"

## Inspector / Owner Details

Review or update:

- **Owner / Inspector Name** — the name associated with your inspection business.
- **Default Signature Name** — the name used for signatures on applicable documents.

## Credentials & Licenses

Enter the professional details relevant to your business:

- **Company Licence Number**
- **Association**
- **Association Number** — your membership or certification number.

<Tip>
  Use the licence and association details that apply to your location and professional membership. Not every inspector will have every credential.
</Tip>

## Standards of Practice

Record the standard your inspections follow:

- **SOP Name** — full name of the standard.
- **SOP Short Name** — abbreviated name.
- **SOP Version / Date** — the applicable version or date.
- **SOP Link** — a link to the published standard, when available.

<Note>
  Confirm that the selected standard and its version are appropriate for the inspections you perform. Your professional obligations may vary by province, association, and service.
</Note>

## Confirm Your Changes

After editing a field, check the **Saved** indicator near the bottom of the page to confirm the changes have been recorded. There is no separate Save button shown on this screen.

Review these details whenever your credentials, association membership, or applicable Standards of Practice change.
