Use **Fees & Services** to maintain the pricing options available when preparing Work Orders.

## Open Fees & Services

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings**.
3. Select **Fees & Services**.

## Inspection Fee & Taxes

Under **Inspection Fee & Taxes**, enter your:

- **General Inspection Fee** — your standard inspection price.
- **Tax Rate (%)** — the applicable tax percentage.
- **Tax Code Name** — the label used for that tax, such as HST where applicable.

## Set a Default Discount

In **Default Discount**, choose **\$ Dollar** or **% Percentage**, then enter the discount amount.

InspectIQ uses this default when a Work Order does **not** have its own discount. A discount entered for an individual Work Order takes precedence.

## Manage Ancillary Services

The **Ancillary Services** list is your editable catalogue of optional services, such as radon testing or thermal imaging.

- Change the **service name** or **price** in its field.
- Use the **ON/OFF** control to show or hide a service on new Work Orders.
- Select the **X** beside a service to remove it from the catalogue.
- Use the add-service control, where available, to create your own service.

<Tip>
  Turn off services you don't currently offer rather than removing them, so they are hidden from new Work Orders.
</Tip>

## Before Creating a Work Order

Check your general inspection fee, tax settings, discount, and optional service prices. These settings provide your starting pricing options; review the amounts on each Work Order before sending anything to a client.
