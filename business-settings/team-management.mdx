Use **Team / Members** to invite inspectors to your team and see the status of outstanding invitations.

## Open Team / Members

1. In the main navigation, select **Settings**.
2. Under **Business Settings**, select **Team / Members**.
3. Review the members and pending invitations shown on the page.

<img
  src="/images/01-team-members.jpg"
  alt="01 Team Members"
  title="01 Team Members"
/>

The page also shows your team seat allowance and usage.

## Invite a Team Member

1. Select **\+ Invite Member**.
2. Enter the inspector's email address in the **Invite Team Member** dialog.
3. Select **Send Invite**.

<img
  src="/images/02-invite-member.jpg"
  alt="02 Invite Member"
  title="02 Invite Member"
/>

The invitation appears in **Pending Invites** while the recipient has not yet completed registration.

<img
  src="/images/03-pending-invites.jpg"
  alt="03 Pending Invites"
  title="03 Pending Invites"
/>

To withdraw an outstanding invitation, select **Cancel** beside it.

## How an Invited Inspector Joins

The inspector receives a **Team Invite** email containing a sign-up link. They can follow the link to the **Accept Invite** page and complete the required account details.

<img
  src="/images/04-invitation-email.jpg"
  alt="04 Invitation Email"
  title="04 Invitation Email"
/>

<img
  src="/images/05-accept-invite.jpg"
  alt="05 Accept Invite"
  title="05 Accept Invite"
/>

<Tip>
  Ask the inspector to use the email address that received the invitation. If they cannot find the message, have them check their spam or junk folder.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The recording shows inviting a member, tracking a pending invitation, cancelling an invitation, and the invitee's registration screen. It does not demonstrate changing roles or managing existing active members.
</Note>
