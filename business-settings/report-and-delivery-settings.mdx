Use **Report & Delivery Settings** to manage how inspection reports are delivered and the permission language used in your agreements.

Go to **Settings → Business Settings → Report & Delivery Settings**.

<img
  src="/images/01-report-delivery-settings.jpg"
  alt="01 Report Delivery Settings"
  title="01 Report Delivery Settings"
/>

## Report Delivery

Under **Report Delivery**, review the following fields:

- **Delivery Method** — The recording shows **Email** as the selected method.
- **Delivery Authorization Language** — Review or edit the wording that authorizes delivery of the completed report.

This is a business-wide setting. It does not send a report by itself. For report sending and recipient selection, see [Managing & Delivering Reports](/reports/managing-delivering-reports).

## Third-Party Sharing Authorization

The **Third-Party Sharing Authorization** field lets you maintain the wording used when clients authorize sharing with others, such as a real estate representative or attorney.

InspectIQ identifies this field as the source for the `{{third_party_authorization}}` agreement token. Review the language alongside your agreement template to make sure it reflects your business's practices.

<Note>
  Report-delivery and third-party-sharing terms can have legal implications. Have your agreement wording reviewed for your jurisdiction and business requirements.
</Note>

## Review your changes

After editing the authorization fields, confirm the page indicates that your changes have been saved before leaving Settings.

<Tip>
  Preview an agreement after changing authorization wording to confirm the final text reads correctly in context. See [Templates & Agreements](/settings/templates-agreements).
</Tip>
