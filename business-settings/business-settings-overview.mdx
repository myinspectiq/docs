Use **Business Settings** to manage your inspection company's information, services, inspection defaults, agreements, and report preferences.

## Open Business Settings

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **Business Settings** at the top of the Settings page.
3. Select a section from the Business Settings menu.

<img
  src="/images/01-business-settings-1.jpg"
  alt="01 Business Settings 1"
  title="01 Business Settings 1"
/>

<Note>
  **Business Settings** manages company-wide configuration. **My Settings** is for your individual profile and preferences.
</Note>

## What's in Business Settings?

| Section | What you can manage |
| --- | --- |
| **Company Profile** | Company information, logo, and contact details. |
| **Fees & Services** | Inspection services, pricing, tax codes, and related fees. |
| **Team / Members** | Your team's members and invitations, where applicable. |
| **Credentials & Standards** | Professional credentials and inspection standards. |
| **Inspection Setup** | Default inspection configuration, Areas / Rooms, and inspection categories. |
| **Templates & Agreements** | Inspection agreement templates and agreement wording. |
| **Inspection Report Content** | Standard wording and content used in inspection reports. |
| **Report & Delivery Settings** | Report delivery preferences and related settings. |
| **Subscription & Billing** | Subscription and billing information. |
| **Integrations** | Available integration settings. |

## Start with Your Company Profile

Review your company name, branding, and contact details so your business information is accurate.

## Configure Your Inspection Defaults

Under **Inspection Setup**, review the default inspection settings and the **Areas / Rooms Library**. These settings help prepare the structure of new inspections.

<img
  src="/images/03-inspection-setup.jpg"
  alt="03 Inspection Setup"
  title="03 Inspection Setup"
/>

<Tip>
  Review your inspection setup before creating your first inspection so the available areas and rooms reflect how you work.
</Tip>

## Review Agreements and Report Preferences

Use **Templates & Agreements** to manage your inspection agreement and its standard wording. Review **Inspection Report Content** and **Report & Delivery Settings** to configure the content and delivery preferences used by your business.

<img
  src="/images/04-templates-agreements.jpg"
  alt="04 Templates Agreements"
  title="04 Templates Agreements"
/>

## Before Your First Inspection

Make sure your company details, services and fees, default inspection setup, and agreement template are ready. You can return to **Business Settings** later to update them as your business needs change.

<Note>
  The settings available to you may depend on your account and team permissions.
</Note>
