Use **Inspection Setup** to configure the structure InspectIQ uses when creating new inspections.

Go to **Settings → Business Settings → Inspection Setup**. The page contains four expandable sections: **Defaults**, **Area / Room Library**, **Inspection Categories**, and **Severity Definitions**.

<img
  src="/images/01-inspection-setup.jpg"
  alt="01 Inspection Setup"
  title="01 Inspection Setup"
/>

## Defaults

Expand **Defaults** to review company-wide settings for new Work Orders and client reminders.

<Note>
  The recording shows the Defaults section but does not open its fields. Review the available options in your account before making changes.
</Note>

## Area / Room Library

Expand **Area / Room Library** to manage the areas and checklists used when creating inspections. Areas are grouped into sections such as **Exterior** and **Interior**.

- Select **Add Area / Room** to create another entry.
- Select **Edit** next to an existing area to change it.
- Select **Delete** to remove an area you no longer want in your library.

<img
  src="/images/02-area-room-library.jpg"
  alt="02 Area Room Library"
  title="02 Area Room Library"
/>

### Add an area or room

1. Select **Add Area / Room**.
2. Enter the **Name**.
3. Choose an **Area Group**.
4. Optionally enter a **Description**.
5. Add any **Checklist Items** you want inspectors to see.
6. Select **Create**.

<img
  src="/images/03-add-area-room.jpg"
  alt="03 Add Area Room"
  title="03 Add Area Room"
/>

<Warning>
  Changes to the Area / Room Library apply to **future inspections only**. Inspections already created retain their own checklist snapshot.
</Warning>

## Inspection Categories

Expand **Inspection Categories** to manage the system classifications used for findings, such as **Plumbing**, **Electrical**, or **Roofing**. Existing categories have **Edit** and **Delete** actions.

To create a category, enter its name in the **Add Category** field and select **Add**.

<img
  src="/images/04-inspection-categories.jpg"
  alt="04 Inspection Categories"
  title="04 Inspection Categories"
/>

<Tip>
  An **Area / Room** describes _where_ you inspected. An **Inspection Category** describes _which system_ a finding relates to.
</Tip>

## Severity Definitions

Expand **Severity Definitions** to review the five severity levels used across findings and reports:

- **Informational** — General information; no action required.
- **Minor** — Minor defect or wear; routine maintenance recommended.
- **Moderate** — Moderate concern; further evaluation and correction recommended.
- **Major** — Major concern; correction by a qualified professional recommended.
- **Safety** — Safety hazard; immediate evaluation by a qualified professional recommended.

<img
  src="/images/05-severity-definitions.jpg"
  alt="05 Severity Definitions"
  title="05 Severity Definitions"
/>

<Note>
  Review your Area / Room Library before creating new inspections so the starting structure reflects your inspection process.
</Note>
