InspectIQ is designed around one connected process: **organize the job, confirm the appointment and agreement, inspect the property, review your findings, and deliver the finished report.**

This guide explains _why_ that sequence works and what to check before moving forward. For button-by-button instructions and screenshots, follow the linked guides at each stage.

<Note>
  **Recommended, not restrictive.** You can create an inspection directly when appropriate. For a typical client-facing inspection involving scheduling and an agreement, starting with a **Work Order** provides the most complete workflow.
</Note>

## Follow the recommended sequence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Work Order">
    Begin when a client requests an inspection. Capture the property address, inspection type, services, client contact details, applicable fees and taxes, and any agent information. On a Team account, assign the Work Order to the appropriate inspector when prompted.

    **Why this comes first:** The Work Order keeps the information needed for scheduling, agreements, and the linked inspection together.

    **Before continuing:** Confirm the property, contact email, services, and final pricing are correct.

    [Creating a Work Order](/work-orders/creating-work-orders) · [Work Orders Overview](/work-orders/work-orders-overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule the inspection">
    Choose the scheduling method that matches the conversation with your client:

    - **Send Time Options to Client** if the client still needs to choose an appointment.
    - **Choose Date & Time Myself** if the date and time have already been agreed upon.

    **Why this comes next:** The agreement and inspection preparation should reflect the actual appointment.

    **Before continuing:** Check that the Work Order shows **Scheduled**. Sending options is not the same as confirming a booking.

    [Scheduling an Inspection](/work-orders/scheduling-inspections)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the inspection agreement">
    Review the agreement for the correct client, property, fees, and terms. Send it for the client's signature, then complete the inspector countersignature step when prompted.

    **Why this matters:** The signing process is part of preparing a client-facing inspection, not something to assume has been completed when an appointment is booked.

    **Before continuing:** Confirm the agreement is **Complete (Signed)** and the Work Order indicates **Inspection Ready**. InspectIQ automatically creates the linked draft inspection as the agreement workflow completes.

    [Inspection Agreements](/work-orders/agreements-signatures)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct the inspection area by area">
    Select **Open Inspection** from the ready Work Order, or find the inspection under **Inspections**. Work through the relevant areas and rooms. Update overviews and document observed conditions as you inspect.

    **Recommended habit:** Capture evidence while you are at the property, rather than relying on memory to reconstruct observations later. A finding is only needed where there is something relevant to report—not for every room.

    [Conducting an Inspection](/inspections/conducting-an-inspection) · [Areas & Rooms](/inspections/area-overviews) · [Capturing Photos & Voice Notes](/inspections/capturing-photos)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use AI to draft findings, then apply your judgement">
    Add photographs and/or voice notes for the condition you observed, then use **Generate AI Finding**. Review the proposed title, category, severity, observation, and recommendation. Edit as needed and **Approve Finding** only when it accurately represents your inspection.

    **Why this matters:** AI helps draft the wording; **the inspector remains responsible for the inspection conclusions**.

    [AI Findings](/inspections/ai-generated-findings)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your inspection review">
    Revisit the inspection workspace. Check that applicable areas and rooms have been reviewed, relevant findings are approved, and supporting photos and descriptions are accurate. Correct missing or inconsistent information before reporting.

    **Before continuing:** Ask yourself whether another inspector could understand the observed condition from the documented evidence and approved finding.

    [Completing an Inspection](/inspections/completing-inspection)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate, review, and finalize your reports">
    Open the **Report Builder** using **Generate Report**. Generate an AI-assisted summary if needed, review and edit its content, and save your changes. Preview **both** the Summary Report and Full Report before finalizing.

    **Important:** If inspection content changes after PDFs have been produced, use **Regenerate Reports**, then review the updated documents again.

    [Generating & Finalizing Reports](/reports/finalizing-reports)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver the right documents to the right people">
    From the report preview, choose the Summary Report, Full Inspection Report, or both. Verify the email recipient, any authorized CC addresses, and the selected documents. Address any applicable payment or release requirements before sending.

    **Your finish line:** The report has been reviewed, the intended versions have been sent, and the delivery state can be tracked under **Reports**.

    [Managing & Delivering Reports](/reports/managing-delivering-reports)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working without a connection?

InspectIQ's offline workflow can help you continue documenting an inspection when connectivity is limited. Plan to reconnect and confirm synchronization before relying on the latest information for report preparation.
