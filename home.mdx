## Your First Inspection, Made Simple

From setting up your business to producing your first professional report, here's how to get started.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Business">
    Configure your account, company information, and inspection preferences.

    [Account Setup Guide](/getting-started/account-setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and Schedule a Work Order">
    Add your client and property details, select inspection services, schedule the appointment, and complete the agreement process.

    [Creating Work Orders](/work-orders/creating-work-orders)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture Your Findings">
