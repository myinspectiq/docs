Your first inspection follows a straightforward path: **create a work order, confirm the appointment and agreement, conduct the inspection, then prepare the report**.

This guide introduces the complete process. For detailed instructions, follow the linked guides at each stage.

## 1. Start from the Dashboard

Sign in and open **Dashboard**. Use **New Work Order** to begin with a client and property, or **New Inspection** when you need to create an inspection directly.

<img
  src="/images/01-dashboard-1.jpg"
  alt="01 Dashboard 1"
  title="01 Dashboard 1"
/>

<Tip>
  For a client-facing inspection with scheduling and an agreement, start with a **Work Order**.
</Tip>

## 2. Create a Work Order

1. Select **New Work Order**.
2. Enter the property information and select the applicable inspection type and services.
3. Add the client and any other required contact details.
4. Review pricing and the remaining fields before continuing.

<img
  src="/images/02-new-work-order.jpg"
  alt="02 New Work Order"
  title="02 New Work Order"
/>

Your work order appears on the **Work Orders** board, where you can return to it as you work through the remaining steps.

<img
  src="/images/03-work-orders-board.jpg"
  alt="03 Work Orders Board"
  title="03 Work Orders Board"
/>

For step-by-step field descriptions, see [Work Orders Overview](/work-orders/work-orders-overview).

## 3. Schedule and prepare the agreement

From the work order, choose the scheduling approach that suits the appointment:

- **Send Time Options to Client** to offer appointment choices.
- **Choose Date & Time Myself** to set the appointment yourself.

Complete the agreement workflow shown in the work order. InspectIQ displays the agreement and signature stages so you can monitor what is still outstanding.

<img
  src="/images/04-agreement-step.jpg"
  alt="04 Agreement Step"
  title="04 Agreement Step"
/>

<Note>
  The work order’s next available action depends on its scheduling and agreement status. Follow the on-screen prompts rather than assuming the inspection is ready immediately after creating the order.
</Note>

## 4. Open the inspection

When the work order reaches **Inspection Ready**, select **Open Inspection**. You can also find your inspection under **Inspections**.

<img
  src="/images/05-open-inspection.jpg"
  alt="05 Open Inspection"
  title="05 Open Inspection"
/>

In the inspection workspace, you can review the property information, report cover photo, and list of inspection areas and rooms.

<img
  src="/images/06-inspection-areas.jpg"
  alt="06 Inspection Areas"
  title="06 Inspection Areas"
/>

## 5. Document an area and capture a finding

1. Open the area or room you want to inspect using **Edit / Inspect**.
2. Review or prepare its **Area / Room Overview**.
3. Select **Capture Finding** when you have a condition to document.
4. Add photos and/or voice notes, then select **Generate AI Finding**.

<img
  src="/images/07-capture-finding.jpg"
  alt="07 Capture Finding"
  title="07 Capture Finding"
/>

On desktop, use **Upload Photo**. On mobile browsers, **Capture Photo** also lets you use the phone camera.

InspectIQ drafts the finding from your evidence. **Review and edit the suggested details before selecting Approve Finding.**

<img
  src="/images/08-review-ai-finding.jpg"
  alt="08 Review Ai Finding"
  title="08 Review Ai Finding"
/>

<Warning>
  AI suggestions are not inspection conclusions. Verify every observation, severity, and recommendation against what you actually inspected.
</Warning>

Repeat this process for the applicable areas. You don't need to create a finding for every room.

For more detail, see [Conducting an Inspection](/inspections/conducting-an-inspection) and [Capturing Photos & Voice Notes](/inspections/capturing-photos).

## 6. Review and prepare your report

After documenting the relevant areas and reviewing your findings, move to the **Report Builder**. Review the generated report content and available summary controls before finalizing your report.

<img
  src="/images/09-report-builder.jpg"
  alt="09 Report Builder"
  title="09 Report Builder"
/>

Preview both the **Summary Report** and **Full Report** to check the final content. When ready, use the reporting workflow to generate and deliver the appropriate files.

For the full process, see [Generating & Finalizing Reports](/reports/finalizing-reports) and [Managing & Delivering Reports](/reports/managing-delivering-reports).

<Tip>
  Before sending a report, check the property details, approved findings, photos, recommendations, and report previews for accuracy.
</Tip>
