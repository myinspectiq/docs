Ready to complete your first inspection with InspectIQ?

This guide walks you through the entire process, from creating a Work Order and scheduling the appointment to capturing findings, generating reports, and delivering them to your client.

<Note>
  Before starting, we recommend completing your **Business Settings**, including your inspection fees, inspection defaults, agreement template, and report content.
</Note>

## Create a Work Order

Every inspection begins with a Work Order.

1. Select **Work Orders** from the main navigation.
2. Select **\+ New Work Order**.
3. Enter the property's address and available property information.
4. Select the inspection type and any additional services.
5. Review the inspection fees, taxes, and discounts.
6. Enter your client's contact information.
7. Select **Create Work Order**.

If you're using a Team account, you may also need to assign the Work Order to an inspector.

<Tip>
  Use the property lookup feature to retrieve available property details and reduce manual entry. Always verify the information before proceeding.
</Tip>

## Schedule the Inspection

Once you've created your Work Order, you'll need to schedule the appointment.

InspectIQ provides two options:

### Send Date & Time Options

Select **Send Date & Time Options** to offer your client several available appointments.

Your client can choose their preferred date and time from the options you provide.

### Choose Date & Time Myself

Select **Choose Date & Time Myself** if you've already arranged an appointment.

Choose the inspection date and time, then confirm the schedule.

## Prepare the Inspection Agreement

After scheduling, prepare the inspection agreement.

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Select **Generate Agreement**.
3. Review the agreement information.
4. Send the agreement to your client.
5. Monitor the agreement's signature status.
6. Complete the inspector countersignature when required.

InspectIQ uses your configured agreement template and Work Order details to prepare the agreement.

<Note>
  Review your agreement wording under **Business Settings → Templates & Agreements** before sending your first agreement.
</Note>

## Open Your Inspection

Once the required Work Order steps are complete:

1. Open the Work Order.
2. Select **Open Inspection**.

You can also access your inspections through **Inspections** in the main navigation.

Your inspection workspace contains the areas and rooms available for the property.

## Work Through Areas & Rooms

InspectIQ organizes inspections into areas and rooms, such as Exterior, Interior, Roof / Attic, and Mechanical.

To begin inspecting an area:

1. Find the applicable area or room.
2. Select **Edit / Inspect**.
3. Review the Area / Room Overview.
4. Document your observations and findings.

Continue through the applicable areas and rooms as you inspect the property.

## Capture Your Observations

InspectIQ lets you document observations using photos and voice notes.

### Add Photos

**On Desktop:** Select **Upload Photo** to add an image from your computer.

**On Mobile or the Phone App:** Select **Capture Photo** to open your device's camera and take a picture directly.

You can also upload existing images from your device.

### Record Voice Notes

1. Select **Record Voice Note**.
2. Describe the observed condition.
3. Stop the recording when finished.
4. Add another voice note if additional information is needed.

<Tip>
  Describe what you see, where it's located, and why it may require attention. Clear observations help InspectIQ generate more accurate findings.
</Tip>

## Generate AI Findings

Once you've captured your observation:

1. Select **Generate AI Finding**.
2. Review the suggested title, severity, and inspection category.
3. Review the observation and recommendation.
4. Make any necessary changes.
5. Select **Approve Finding**.

If the suggested wording isn't suitable, use **Regenerate AI** to request another version.

<Warning>
  Always verify AI-generated findings before approving them. You remain responsible for the accuracy of your inspection observations and recommendations.
</Warning>

## Complete Your Inspection Areas

As you finish each area or room:

1. Review the Area / Room Overview.
2. Check that all relevant findings have been documented.
3. Approve the applicable findings.
4. Select **Mark Area Complete**.

Repeat this process for each applicable area or room.

Return to the main inspection page to review your overall progress.

## Generate Your Reports

Once you've finished documenting the inspection:

1. Open the inspection.
2. Select **Generate Report** to access the Report Builder.
3. Review the report title and available summary sections.
4. Select **Generate AI Summary**.
5. Review and edit the generated content.
6. Select **Save Report**.
7. Preview the report documents.
8. Select **Finalize Report** when satisfied.

InspectIQ prepares two report types:

- **Summary Report** — A condensed overview of important findings and concerns.
- **Full Inspection Report** — The detailed inspection findings, photographs, and recommendations.

<Note>
  Saving a report and finalizing a report are separate actions. Always review your content before finalization.
</Note>

## Preview and Deliver Your Reports

Once your reports are finalized:

1. Select **Reports** from the main navigation.
2. Find the report under **Ready to Send**.
3. Select **Preview** to review the documents.
4. Select **Send Report**.
5. Choose the **Summary Report**, **Full Inspection Report**, or both.
6. Verify the client's email address.
7. Add any authorized CC recipients, if needed.
8. Select **Send Report**.

<Note>
  Some reports may require payment before they can be released. Review the payment status if the delivery option is unavailable.
</Note>

## Congratulations — You've Completed Your First Inspection!

You've now worked through the complete InspectIQ inspection workflow:

1. Created a Work Order.
2. Scheduled an appointment.
3. Prepared and signed an inspection agreement.
4. Captured observations and AI-assisted findings.
5. Reviewed and completed your inspection areas.
6. Generated and finalized your reports.
7. Delivered the reports to your client.

As you become more familiar with InspectIQ, your configured defaults, reusable templates, and AI-assisted tools can help reduce repetitive administrative work.

<Tip>
  For future inspections, start with **Work Orders → New Work Order** and follow the same process.
</Tip>
