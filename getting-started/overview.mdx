Welcome to **InspectIQ**, the AI-powered home inspection report writer. Manage inspection appointments, document property conditions with photos and voice notes, and prepare reports with AI assistance—all in one place.

## Get to Know Your Dashboard

After signing in, open **Dashboard** from the main navigation. It gives you a quick view of your daily work, including:

- **Today's Inspections** and **Ready for Review** counts.
- **Reports to Send** and **Agreements Pending**.
- **Today's Schedule** and your next scheduled inspection.
- The **InspectIQ Assistant (Beta)** panel, with links to inspections, Work Orders, and report delivery.

If your account has team access, use **My Work** and **Team** to switch the view.

From the navigation, use **Work Orders** to organize upcoming jobs, **Inspections** to document property conditions, **Reports** to review and deliver PDFs, and **Settings** to configure your account and business.

## Your Inspection Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your account">
    Add your business details and configure fees, inspection defaults, and agreement templates. See [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and schedule a Work Order">
    Enter client and property details, select services, arrange the appointment, and prepare the inspection agreement. See [Creating a Work Order](/work-orders/creating-a-work-order).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct your inspection">
    Open the inspection, work through applicable areas and rooms, and document observations with photos or voice notes. See [Conducting an Inspection](/inspections/conducting-an-inspection).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review AI-assisted findings">
    Check and, where needed, edit the suggested observation, recommendation, category, and severity before approving. See [AI Findings](/inspections/ai-findings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare and deliver reports">
    Review the **Summary Report** and **Full Inspection Report**, then deliver the appropriate documents to your client. See [Reports Overview](/reports/reports-overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find and Continue Your Inspections

Select **Inspections** to see work grouped by stage, including **Not Started**, **In Progress**, **Ready for Review**, **Finalized**, and **Delivered**. You can search and filter the list, then select the action shown on an inspection card to start, continue, or view its report.

## Use InspectIQ on Site

- **Desktop:** Upload existing photos and manage inspection details and reports.
- **Mobile browser:** Use **Capture Photo** to open your device camera, or **Upload Photo** to select an existing image. You can also record voice notes.
- **Native mobile apps:** App Store and Google Play listings are **COMING SOON**. Until then, use the supported mobile browser experience.

<Note>
  The planned app address is **app.myinspectiq.com**. Use the access link provided for your account until the production address is confirmed.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Before your first job, check your company profile, inspection fees, areas and rooms, agreement template, and report content under **Settings → Business Settings**.
</Tip>
