Welcome to InspectIQ — the AI-powered home inspection platform built specifically for home inspectors. Whether you're a solo operator or running a busy multi-inspector firm, InspectIQ helps you capture findings on-site, generate professional reports in minutes, and deliver a polished experience to every client. This section walks you through everything you need to get up and running quickly.
