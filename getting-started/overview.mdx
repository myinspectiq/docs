Welcome to **InspectIQ**, the AI-powered home inspection report writer.

InspectIQ helps you manage your inspection workflow, capture observations using photos and voice notes, and generate professional inspection reports with AI assistance.

Whether you're an independent inspector or part of a team, this guide will help you get started.

## Your Inspection Workflow

InspectIQ brings together the key steps of a home inspection in one platform.

### 1. Set Up Your Account

Start by entering your company information and configuring your inspection preferences.

This includes your business details, inspection fees, areas and rooms, agreement templates, and report settings.

[Set Up Your Account](/getting-started/account-setup)

### 2. Create a Work Order

Enter the property information, client details, inspection services, and pricing.

You can then schedule the appointment and prepare the inspection agreement for electronic signatures.

[Creating a Work Order](/work-orders/creating-work-orders)

### 3. Conduct Your Inspection

Open your inspection and work through the applicable areas and rooms.

Capture photos, record voice notes, and document observations as you inspect the property.

InspectIQ uses your information to help prepare structured inspection findings.

[Conducting an Inspection](/untitled-page-6)

### 4. Review Your AI Findings

Review the AI-generated observations, recommendations, categories, and severity levels.

You can edit, regenerate, or approve findings before including them in your inspection documentation.

<Note>
  You remain responsible for reviewing and approving the accuracy of your inspection findings.
</Note>

### 5. Generate Your Reports

Use the Report Builder to review your inspection content, generate AI-assisted summaries, and prepare your reports.

InspectIQ provides two report types:

- **Summary Report** — Highlights important findings and concerns.
- **Full Inspection Report** — Provides the detailed inspection findings, photographs, and recommendations.

[Reports Overview](/untitled-page-7)

### 6. Deliver Your Reports

Once your reports are finalized, you can review and deliver the Summary Report, Full Inspection Report, or both to your client.

InspectIQ also lets you monitor report delivery and payment status.

## Using InspectIQ on Different Devices

You can access InspectIQ through your desktop browser or supported mobile experience.

**On Desktop**

Upload existing inspection photos, manage Work Orders, review findings, and prepare reports.

**On Mobile**

Use **Capture Photo** to open your device's camera and photograph inspection conditions directly.

You can also record voice notes and document findings while working through the property.

<Tip>
  Many inspectors may find it convenient to capture observations on-site using their phone and review their reports later on a larger screen.
</Tip>

## Before Your First Inspection

We recommend reviewing these settings before getting started:

- **Company Profile** — Your business name, logo, and contact information.
- **Fees & Services** — Your inspection pricing, taxes, and additional services.
- **Inspection Setup** — Your default inspection settings, areas, rooms, and categories.
- **Templates & Agreements** — Your inspection agreement wording and templates.
- **Inspection Report Content** — Your standard report language and limitations.

You can access these options under **Settings → Business Settings**.

[Explore Business Settings](/settings/business-settings)

Once your account and business settings are configured, you're ready to create your first Work Order and begin inspecting.
