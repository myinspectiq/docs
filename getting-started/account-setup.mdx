Getting started with InspectIQ begins by choosing how you'll use the platform. You can register as an individual inspector, set up a team, or join a team using an invitation.

## Choose how to create your account

On the sign-in screen, select **Sign up** beneath **Sign in**. Then choose the option that matches your situation:

- **Individual Inspector** — Create an account to manage your own inspections, capture findings, and generate AI-assisted reports.
- **Create a Team** — Register as a team owner to manage inspectors, assign work orders, and oversee reports.
- **I Have an Invite** — Join an existing team using the invitation token from your email.

## Register as an Individual Inspector

1. Choose **Individual Inspector**.
2. Enter your **Full Name** and **Email**.
3. Create and confirm your **Password**.
4. Select **Create account**.

<img
  src="/images/02-individual-inspector.jpg"
  alt="02 Individual Inspector"
  title="02 Individual Inspector"
/>

## Create a Team Account

If you operate an inspection business with multiple inspectors:

1. Select **Create a Team**.
2. Enter your **Team / Company Name**.
3. If you will also perform inspections yourself, use the **I also want to perform inspections myself** checkbox, as appropriate.
4. Complete the owner's name, email, password, and password confirmation.
5. Select **Create account**.

<img
  src="/images/03-team-owner.jpg"
  alt="03 Team Owner"
  title="03 Team Owner"
/>

## Join a team with an Invitation

1. Select **I Have an Invite**.
2. Enter the **Invite Token** from your invitation email.
3. Complete your name, email, password, and password confirmation.
4. Select **Create account**.

<img
  src="/images/04-accept-invite.jpg"
  alt="04 Accept Invite"
  title="04 Accept Invite"
/>

<Note>
  If you already have an account, select **Sign in** instead of creating another one. The sign-in screen also provides a **Forgot password?** option.
</Note>
