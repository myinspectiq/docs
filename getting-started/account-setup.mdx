Getting started with InspectIQ is simple. Whether you're an independent home inspector, managing a team, or joining an existing inspection business, you can create an account that fits your role.

## Create Your Account

1. Open the [InspectIQ application](https://app.myinspectiq.com/).
2. Select **Sign Up**.
3. Choose how you'd like to use InspectIQ.

You'll see three registration options:

- **Individual Inspector**
- **Create a Team**
- **I Have an Invite**

## Individual Inspector

Choose **Individual Inspector** if you conduct inspections independently.

To register:

1. Select **Individual Inspector**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**.
3. Enter your **Email**.
4. Create a **Password**.
5. Re-enter your password in **Confirm Password**.
6. Select **Create account**.

Once registered, you can proceed with configuring your business and inspection settings.

## Create a Team

Choose **Create a Team** if you manage an inspection business with multiple inspectors.

This account type allows you to manage inspectors, assign work orders, and oversee inspection reports.

### Register Your Team

1. Select **Create a Team**.
2. Enter your **Team / Company Name**.
3. Enter your **Full Name**.
4. Enter your **Email**.
5. Create and confirm your password.
6. Select **Create account**.

### If You Also Perform Inspections

During registration, you'll see the option:

**I also want to perform inspections myself**

Enable this checkbox if you plan to conduct inspections in addition to managing your team.

<Note>
  A Team Owner who also performs inspections counts toward the team's available inspector seats. You can manage this preference later under **My Settings → My Profile → Inspection Role**.
</Note>

## Join an Existing Team

If you've received an invitation from an inspection business, choose **I Have an Invite**.

### Accept Your Invitation

1. Select **I Have an Invite**.
2. Enter the **Invite Token** provided in your invitation email.
3. Enter your **Full Name**.
4. Enter your **Email**.
5. Create and confirm your password.
6. Select **Create account**.

<Tip>
  Use the email address associated with your invitation to avoid registration issues.
</Tip>

## Other Sign-In Options

InspectIQ also displays options to continue with:

- **Google**
- **Facebook**

These options appear on the registration and sign-in screens.

## Sign In to InspectIQ

If you already have an account:

1. Open the [InspectIQ application](https://app.myinspectiq.com/).
2. Enter your registered **Email**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If you've forgotten your password, select **Forgot password?** and follow the recovery instructions.

## Set Up Your Business

After creating your account, we recommend reviewing your Business Settings before creating your first Work Order.

Go to **Settings → Business Settings**.

Start with:

- **Company Profile** — Add your company name, logo, and contact information.
- **Fees & Services** — Configure inspection pricing, taxes, and additional services.
- **Credentials & Standards** — Enter applicable professional credentials and Standards of Practice.
- **Inspection Setup** — Review inspection defaults, areas, rooms, and categories.
- **Templates & Agreements** — Configure the agreement used with your Work Orders.
- **Inspection Report Content** — Review the wording used in your reports.

[Explore Business Settings](/settings/business-settings)

## Review Your Personal Settings

Next, go to **Settings → My Settings**.

Here you can manage:

- **My Profile** — Your name, contact details, profile photo, and inspection role.
- **Inspection Preferences** — Your local photo-saving preference.
- **Security** — Your account password.

These settings apply to your individual account.

## Ready for Your First Inspection?

Once you've configured your account and business preferences, you're ready to create your first Work Order.

Your next steps are to enter the property and client information, schedule the inspection, prepare the agreement, and begin inspecting.

[Walk Through Your First Inspection](/getting-started/first-inspection)

<Tip>
  Taking a few minutes to configure your business settings now will save repetitive work when creating future Work Orders and inspection reports.
</Tip>
