My Profile lets you manage your personal information, profile photo, and inspection role within InspectIQ.

These settings are specific to your account and are separate from your company's Business Settings.

## Access My Profile

1. Select **Settings** from the main navigation.
2. Choose **My Settings**.
3. Select **My Profile** from the left-hand menu.

## Profile Photo

Your profile photo identifies you within InspectIQ and may be visible to your Team Owner and administrators.

### Add or Change Your Photo

1. Locate the **Profile Photo** section.
2. Select **Change Photo**.
3. Choose an image from your device.

InspectIQ supports PNG and JPG images and resizes them to 512 pixels before uploading.

For best results, use a square image, approximately 400 × 400 pixels.

To delete your existing photo, select **Remove Photo**.

## Personal Information

Keep your personal contact information accurate and up to date.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Full Name** | Your name as displayed within InspectIQ. |
| **Phone** | Your personal or business contact number. |
| **Email** | The email address associated with your account. |

Update the applicable fields when your information changes.

InspectIQ displays a saved confirmation beneath your personal information when changes have been saved.

<Note>
  Your personal information is associated with your individual account. Company-wide contact details are managed separately under Business Settings.
</Note>

## Account Details

The **Account Details** section displays your account type and role within InspectIQ.

For example, a business owner managing multiple inspectors may have the **Team Owner** account type.

Your account type determines which business management features are available to you.

## Inspection Role

Team Owners can choose whether they personally perform inspections.

The **I perform inspections** toggle controls whether the Team Owner also participates as an inspector.

### Enable Your Inspection Role

1. Scroll to **Inspection Role**.
2. Turn ON **I perform inspections**.

When enabled:

- You can inspect your own Work Orders.
- You count toward your subscription's available team seats.

### Disable Your Inspection Role

If you manage your inspection business but don't personally perform inspections, you can turn OFF **I perform inspections**.

Disabling this option frees up your inspector seat for another team member.

<Warning>
  You cannot turn off your inspection role while you have inspections in progress. Complete any in-progress work before attempting to free your seat.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  If you primarily manage your inspection business and have other inspectors conducting inspections, disabling your inspection role can help make better use of your available team seats.
</Tip>

## Review Your Profile

Before conducting your first inspection, confirm that:

- Your full name is accurate.
- Your phone number and email address are current.
- Your profile photo is up to date.
- Your inspection role is enabled if you personally conduct inspections.

You can return to My Profile whenever your personal information or responsibilities change.
