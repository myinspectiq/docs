My Settings is where you manage everything specific to your personal InspectIQ account — your profile details, login credentials, notification preferences, and the devices you're signed in on. These settings are personal to you and don't affect other team members or your business-wide configuration.

<Note>
  Settings like your business name, logo, and report template are managed in **Business Settings**, not here. My Settings covers your individual account only.
</Note>

To access My Settings, tap your **profile avatar** or **Settings** in the navigation menu and select **My Settings**.

## Personal Profile

Your personal profile contains the details that identify you within InspectIQ — particularly relevant if you work as part of a team.

- **Full Name** — Your name as it appears in work order assignments, report sign-offs, and team activity logs.
- **Email Address** — The email address used to log in and receive account notifications. See [Change Your Email](#change-your-password-or-email) below to update this.
- **Phone Number** — Your personal contact number.
- **Profile Photo** — Upload a photo of yourself (optional). This appears in team views and on assigned work orders.

Tap **Edit Profile** to update any of these fields, then tap **Save**.

## Change Your Password or Email

To update your login credentials:

1. In **My Settings**, tap **Security**.
2. To change your **password**, tap **Change Password**, enter your current password, then enter and confirm your new password. Tap **Save**.
3. To change your **email address**, tap **Change Email**, enter your new email address, and confirm the change via the verification link sent to your new address.

Use a strong, unique password and update it if you ever think your account has been accessed without your knowledge.

## Notification Preferences

Control which notifications InspectIQ sends to you personally. This is separate from the client and team notifications configured in Business Settings — these settings only affect what you receive.

You can toggle notifications on or off for:

- **Work order assigned to you** — Get notified when a job is assigned to your account.
- **Inspection reminders** — Receive a reminder before an upcoming inspection.
- **Payment updates** — Get notified when a payment is received on one of your jobs.
- **Report viewed by client** — Know when a client opens a report you delivered.
- **Team activity** — Receive updates about team members' job completions (Owners and Administrators only).

For each notification, you can choose to receive it by **In-App**, **Email**, or **SMS** (or any combination).

## Connected Devices and Sessions

The **Devices & Sessions** section shows every device that is currently signed in to your InspectIQ account. This helps you spot any unfamiliar activity.

- Each session shows the **device type**, **approximate location**, and **last active time**.
- To sign out of a specific device, tap **Sign Out** next to that session.
- To sign out of all other devices at once, tap **Sign Out of All Other Devices**.

This is useful if you've lost a device, changed phones, or simply want to make sure no old devices still have access to your account.

## App Display Preferences

Adjust how InspectIQ looks and feels on your device:

- **Dark Mode** — Switch between Light, Dark, or System Default (matches your device's system setting).
- **Font Size** — Choose from Small, Medium (default), or Large to make on-screen text easier to read during inspections.

These preferences are stored per device, so you can have different display settings on your phone and tablet.

## Delete Your Account

If you need to permanently close your InspectIQ account, you can do so from **My Settings → Account → Delete Account**.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your personal data, work orders, reports, photos, and inspection history will be deleted and cannot be recovered. If you're part of a team, contact your team Owner before deleting your account — your assigned work orders will need to be reassigned first.
</Warning>

Before deleting, consider downloading a copy of your data from **My Settings → Account → Export My Data**. This gives you a local archive of your inspection history in case you need it in the future.
