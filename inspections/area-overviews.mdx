InspectIQ organizes your inspections into **Areas & Rooms**, making it easy to document different parts of a property and keep track of your progress.

Each area or room has its own overview and findings, allowing you to organize your observations as you move through the property.

## Access Areas & Rooms

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you want to work on.
3. Scroll to the list of areas and rooms.

Areas and rooms are organized into groups such as:

- Exterior
- Interior
- Roof / Attic
- Mechanical

Each group contains the areas or rooms available for that inspection.

## Find an Area or Room

Use the controls above the area list to quickly find what you're looking for.

- **Search** — Find an area or room by name.
- **All Areas** — Filter the list by area group.
- **Add Area / Room** — Add another area or room to the inspection.

To open an area or room, select **Edit / Inspect** beside its name.

## Understanding Area & Room Status

Each area or room displays status indicators to help you track inspection progress.

These indicators help distinguish areas that haven't been started from those where inspection content has been reviewed or approved.

Return to the main inspection page at any time to review your progress across the property.

## Add an Area or Room

You can add areas or rooms to an individual inspection when needed.

For example, a property may have an additional garage, basement, or other space you want to document separately.

### Add an Area or Room to Your Inspection

1. Open the inspection.
2. Select **\+ Add Area / Room**.
3. Configure the new area or room using the available options.
4. Confirm the addition.

The new area or room will appear in your inspection list.

<Note>
  Adding an area or room to an individual inspection is different from updating your company's Area / Room Library under Business Settings.
</Note>

## Remove an Area or Room

If an area or room isn't applicable to the property:

1. Find it in the inspection list.
2. Select **Remove** beside the area or room.

Only remove areas that are genuinely not applicable to the inspection.

## Area / Room Overview

Each area or room includes an **Area / Room Overview** section.

An overview describes the general characteristics or conditions of that area.

For example, a Grounds overview might describe the property's grading, drainage, walkways, or surrounding conditions.

Overviews are separate from individual inspection findings.

### Create an Overview

1. Open the area or room using **Edit / Inspect**.
2. Locate **Area / Room Overview**.
3. Record a voice note describing your observations or use the available upload options.
4. Select **Generate Overview** to prepare an AI-assisted description.
5. Review the generated content.

<Tip>
  Describe the general characteristics of the area clearly. Save specific defects or concerns for individual findings.
</Tip>

## Review and Approve an Overview

InspectIQ allows you to review AI-generated overview content before approving it.

Available actions include:

- **Approve Overview** — Accept the reviewed description.
- **Edit** — Manually adjust the overview wording.
- **Regenerate** — Request a new AI-generated version.
- **Re-record** — Record a replacement voice note.

### Edit an Overview

1. Open the area or room.
2. Review the generated overview.
3. Select **Edit**.
4. Make any necessary changes.
5. Save or approve the updated content using the available controls.

### Regenerate an Overview

If the generated wording doesn't accurately reflect your observations:

1. Select **Regenerate**.
2. Review the new version.
3. Edit it if necessary.
4. Select **Approve Overview** when satisfied.

<Warning>
  Always verify AI-generated content before approving it. Only include information supported by your actual inspection observations.
</Warning>

## Add Supporting Photos

The Area / Room Overview also provides an option to upload supporting photos.

Select **Upload Photo** to attach an image from your device.

On supported mobile devices, InspectIQ also provides camera capture functionality for taking inspection photos directly.

<Note>
  Overview photos and individual finding photos serve different purposes. Use finding photos when documenting a specific defect or concern.
</Note>

## Working with Findings

Below the Area / Room Overview, you'll find the **Findings** section.

This is where you document individual observations, deficiencies, and concerns associated with that area or room.

Select **Capture Finding** to begin documenting a new finding.

You can add supporting photos, record voice notes, and generate an AI-assisted finding for review.

<Tip>
  An area or room may contain an approved overview without having any individual findings. You only need to create findings when there are relevant observations to document.
</Tip>

## Return to the Inspection

When you've finished working on an area or room:

1. Return to the main inspection page.
2. Review the area's updated status.
3. Open the next applicable area or room.
4. Continue documenting the property.

Repeat this process until you've addressed the applicable areas of the inspection.

## Customize Your Default Areas & Rooms

If you regularly inspect properties with similar layouts or want to customize your standard inspection structure, you can manage your default library under:

**Settings → Business Settings → Inspection Setup → Area / Room Library**

Changes to the business-wide library apply to future inspections.

<Note>
  Existing inspections retain the area and room configuration captured when they were created. Updating your default library does not automatically change existing inspections.
</Note>
