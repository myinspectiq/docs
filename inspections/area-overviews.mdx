InspectIQ organizes each inspection by **Areas & Rooms**, so you can document the property one space at a time. Each area has its own **overview** and **findings**.

## Open an Area or Room

1. Go to **Inspections** and open your inspection.
2. Find the **Areas & Rooms** list in the inspection workspace.
3. Select the area or room you want to inspect.

<img
  src="/images/01-inspection-areas.jpg"

  title="01 Inspection Areas"
/>

You can use **Search** to find an area by name, filter the list by group, or select **Add Area / Room** when an additional space needs to be documented.

## Understand the Area Workspace

Inside an area, you will find two main sections:

- **Area / Room Overview:** General characteristics and observations about the space.
- **Findings:** Individual conditions, defects, or recommendations that should be documented separately.

<img
  src="/images/03-area-room-workspace.jpg"
  alt="03 Area Room Workspace"
  title="03 Area Room Workspace"
/>

<Tip>
  A general overview and a specific finding serve different purposes. For example, describe the overall grading of the grounds in the overview, but document a specific drainage concern as a finding.
</Tip>

## Create an Area / Room Overview

1. Open the applicable area or room.
2. In **Area / Room Overview**, use the available voice-recording controls to describe the space.
3. Generate the AI-assisted overview when your recorded information is ready.
4. Read the suggested text and correct anything that does not match your observations.
5. Select **Approve Overview** when the description is accurate.

You can **Edit**, **Regenerate**, or **Re-record** an overview using the controls available for its current state. You may also add supporting photos when applicable.

<Note>
  An overview does not need to contain a defect. Only create an individual finding when there is an observation you need to communicate separately.
</Note>

## Add or Remove Areas

Use **Add Area / Room** on the inspection page to include a space that is not already listed. Where a **Remove** control is provided, use it only for an area that does not apply to the property.

These changes apply to the inspection you are working on. To change your default areas and rooms for future inspections, visit **Business Settings → Inspection Setup → Area / Room Library**.

## Track Your Progress

Return to the main inspection workspace to review the areas you have worked on and continue to the next room or space.

<img
  src="/images/06-inspection-progress.jpg"
  alt="06 Inspection Progress"
  title="06 Inspection Progress"
/>

Before moving on, confirm that any AI-assisted overview reflects what you actually observed and that relevant findings have been reviewed.

For detailed instructions about photo capture, voice recordings, and approving individual findings, see [Capturing Photos & Voice Notes](/inspections/capturing-photos-and-voice-notes) and [AI Findings](/inspections/ai-findings).

<Warning>
  InspectIQ assists with documentation; the inspector is responsible for verifying and approving the content of their inspection.
</Warning>
