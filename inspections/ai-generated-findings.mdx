InspectIQ uses your photos and voice notes to suggest structured findings. **You review and approve every AI-generated finding** before it becomes part of your inspection.

## Generate an AI Finding

1. Open your inspection and select **Edit / Inspect** for the relevant area or room.
2. Select **Capture Finding**.
3. Add photos, voice notes, or both.
4. Select **Generate AI Finding** once you have captured supporting evidence.

<Note>
  The **Generate AI Finding** button is unavailable until you add at least one photo or voice note. For capture instructions, see [Capturing Photos & Voice Notes](/inspections/capturing-photos).
</Note>

## Review the AI Suggestion

The AI suggestion appears **on the same Capture Finding page**. Its status indicates that inspector review is required.

<img
  src="/images/02-ai-suggested.jpg"
  alt="02 Ai Suggested"
  title="02 Ai Suggested"
/>

Check the following fields and adjust them as needed:

- **Finding Title** — a clear description of the condition.
- **Severity** — Informational, Minor, Moderate, Major, or Safety.
- **Inspection Category** — the relevant building system or category.
- **Observation** — what was actually seen during the inspection.
- **Recommendation** — what action, if any, should be taken.
- **Maintenance Recommendation** — ongoing care or monitoring, where applicable.
- **Disclaimer Note** — any relevant limitation or context.

All shown text fields can be edited before approval.

## Choose What Appears in the Summary Report

Use **Include in Summary Report** to control whether a finding is included in the summary. The screen notes that **Safety**, **Major**, and **Moderate** findings are included by default.

Make sure the severity and summary selection reflect the importance of the actual condition—not just the AI suggestion.

## Approve, Regenerate, or Delete

After reviewing the suggestion, choose the appropriate action:

- **Approve Finding** — accept the finding after making any necessary edits.
- **Regenerate AI** — request a refreshed suggestion if the wording needs improvement or the evidence has changed.
- **Delete** — remove the suggested finding.

<Warning>
  AI is an assistant, not the inspector. Check every statement against your own observations. Correct or remove inaccurate, unsupported, or out-of-scope content before approval.
</Warning>

## Continue the Inspection

After approving a finding, return to the relevant area or room and continue capturing findings where needed. You do not need to generate a finding for every area or room.

To see the full inspection workflow, return to [Conducting an Inspection](/inspections/conducting-an-inspection).
