InspectIQ uses AI to help turn your inspection photos and voice notes into structured, professional findings.

Instead of manually writing every observation and recommendation, you can document what you see and let InspectIQ prepare suggested report content for your review.

**You remain in control.** AI-generated findings can be reviewed, edited, regenerated, or deleted before approval.

## Generate an AI Finding

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you're working on.
3. Locate the appropriate area or room.
4. Select **Edit / Inspect**.
5. Under **Findings**, select **Capture Finding**.
6. Add your inspection photos and/or voice notes.
7. Select **Generate AI Finding**.

InspectIQ uses your supplied information to prepare a suggested finding.

<Tip>
  For better results, provide clear photos and describe the location, observed condition, and any concerns in your voice note.
</Tip>

## Understanding an AI Finding

An AI-generated finding contains several fields that help organize your observation and prepare it for the inspection report.

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | A short description of the condition or concern. |
| **Severity** | The importance or urgency assigned to the finding. |
| **Category** | The building system associated with the finding. |
| **Observation** | A description of the condition observed during the inspection. |
| **Recommendation** | Suggested corrective action or further evaluation. |
| **Maintenance Recommendation** | Additional maintenance guidance, where applicable. |
| **Disclaimer** | Relevant limitations or qualifications associated with the finding. |

InspectIQ uses AI to suggest content based on the information you've provided.

## Review Your Finding

Always review an AI-generated finding before approving it.

Check that:

- The title accurately describes the condition.
- The severity reflects the concern.
- The category is appropriate.
- The observation matches what you actually inspected.
- The recommendation is reasonable and appropriate.
- Any maintenance guidance or disclaimers are relevant.

<Warning>
  AI-generated findings may contain inaccuracies or unsupported assumptions. The inspector is responsible for verifying all observations and recommendations before approving them.
</Warning>

## Edit a Finding

If part of a generated finding needs to be changed, you can edit the available fields.

1. Open the generated finding.
2. Select the field you want to modify.
3. Update the wording or selected value.
4. Review the completed finding.
5. Approve it when satisfied.

You can adjust the finding's title, severity, category, observation, recommendation, and other available content.

## Understanding Severity Levels

Severity helps communicate the importance of a finding.

InspectIQ includes five severity levels:

| Severity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Informational** | General information that does not require corrective action. |
| **Minor** | A minor defect or maintenance concern. |
| **Moderate** | A condition that may require further evaluation or correction. |
| **Major** | A significant concern requiring professional attention. |
| **Safety** | A potential safety hazard that may require immediate evaluation. |

The suggested severity can be reviewed and adjusted before approving a finding.

<Note>
  Severity definitions are configured under **Settings → Business Settings → Inspection Setup**.
</Note>

## Inspection Categories

Each finding is assigned an inspection category to identify the building system involved.

Examples include:

- Structural
- Roofing
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Heating & Cooling (HVAC)
- Interior

Review the suggested category and change it if necessary.

Categories are managed under **Business Settings → Inspection Setup → Inspection Categories**.

## Regenerate an AI Finding

If the generated content doesn't accurately reflect your observation, you can request a new version.

1. Open the generated finding.
2. Select **Regenerate AI**.
3. Review the newly generated content.
4. Make any necessary edits.
5. Approve the finding when satisfied.

<Tip>
  If the AI repeatedly misses an important detail, consider adding a clearer voice note or additional supporting information before generating the finding again.
</Tip>

## Approve a Finding

Once you've reviewed and verified the information:

1. Open the finding.
2. Confirm that the content is accurate.
3. Select **Approve Finding**.

Approval indicates that you've reviewed and accepted the finding's content.

Repeat this process for each finding you want included in your inspection documentation.

## Delete a Finding

If a finding is unnecessary, incorrect, or no longer applicable:

1. Locate the finding.
2. Select **Delete**.
3. Follow any confirmation prompts shown.

Only retain findings that accurately represent your inspection observations.

## Working with Multiple Findings

You can document multiple findings within the same area or room.

For each separate concern:

1. Select **Capture Finding**.
2. Add the relevant photos and voice notes.
3. Generate the AI finding.
4. Review and approve the content.

Keeping separate concerns in individual findings helps make your report easier for clients to understand.

## Before Completing Your Inspection

Review your findings to ensure:

- All relevant concerns have been documented.
- Each finding is assigned the appropriate severity and category.
- Observations accurately describe the conditions inspected.
- Recommendations are clear and appropriate.
- Supporting photos are relevant.
- AI-generated content has been reviewed and approved.

<Note>
  InspectIQ assists with preparing inspection findings, but it does not replace the inspector's professional judgment or responsibility for the final report.
</Note>
