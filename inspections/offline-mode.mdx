InspectIQ lets you continue documenting an inspection when Wi-Fi or mobile service is unavailable. Your offline changes are stored on your device and synchronized when you reconnect.

## What Works Offline

When you have already opened the inspection while connected, you can:

- Capture and attach photos.
- Add and edit observations and findings.
- Document area and room overviews.
- Continue working through the inspection and mark it complete.
- Review work orders that were previously loaded on your device.

## What Requires an Internet Connection

The following actions require connectivity:

- **Generate AI Findings** — AI suggestions are generated online.
- **Create new Work Orders** — New jobs must be created while connected.
- **Process payments** — Online payment processing requires internet access.
- **Deliver reports** — Email, SMS, and online report sharing require a connection where supported.

## Prepare Before Going Offline

1. Open InspectIQ while connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data.
2. Go to **Inspections** and open the inspection you plan to conduct.
3. Confirm the inspection and its areas and rooms have loaded before leaving the connection.

<Tip>
  Open your upcoming inspections while you're online, preferably before travelling to a property with unreliable reception.
</Tip>

## Continue Your Inspection

Work through your areas and rooms as usual. Capture photos and record observations while offline. You can prepare your inspection evidence now and use online AI features when your connection returns.

<Note>
  Your session can remain signed in for up to seven days. For best results, sign in and open the inspection while you're online before travelling to the property.
</Note>

## Sync When You Reconnect

When your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, InspectIQ automatically begins synchronizing pending inspection changes, including findings and photos.

1. Keep InspectIQ open after reconnecting.
2. Allow any **Syncing** or **Pending Sync** indicator to clear.
3. Confirm your recent changes appear before closing the app or switching devices.

<Warning>
  Don't close InspectIQ or clear your browser/app data while a sync is pending. Wait until synchronization finishes to reduce the risk of unsent changes.
</Warning>

Once your inspection is synchronized, you can continue to [Generating & Finalizing Reports](/reports/finalizing-reports).
