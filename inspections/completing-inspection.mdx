When you've visited every area, captured all your photos, and reviewed your findings, it's time to mark the inspection as complete and generate the client report. InspectIQ guides you through a pre-completion checklist to make sure nothing is missing, then lets you preview the finished report before it ever reaches your client's inbox.

## What Happens When You Mark an Inspection as Complete

Marking an inspection **Complete** does the following:

- **Locks the inspection status** — the inspection moves from *In Progress* to *Complete* in your Dashboard.
- **Triggers report generation** — InspectIQ compiles all your findings, photos, recommendations, and area summaries into a formatted report.
- **Enables delivery options** — once complete, you can preview, download (PDF), or send the report directly to your client from within the app.
- **Timestamps the report** — the completion date and time are recorded and printed on the report cover page.

The inspection remains accessible for your records and can be re-opened if you need to make changes after the fact (see [Re-opening an Inspection](#re-opening-an-inspection) below).

---

## Pre-Completion Checklist

Before marking complete, InspectIQ automatically checks for the following. You can also review each item manually in the **Checklist** view.

<Tip>
  Tap the **Checklist** icon at the top of your inspection overview screen before you mark it complete. The checklist gives you a color-coded summary of every requirement so you can spot and fix any gaps in seconds—without hunting through areas one by one.
</Tip>

| Checklist Item | What InspectIQ Checks |
|---|---|
| **All areas visited** | Every area added to the inspection has at least one photo or finding logged. |
| **Required photos captured** | Areas with mandatory photo requirements (set in your template) have the minimum number of photos. |
| **All findings reviewed** | No findings remain in *Pending AI Suggestions* or are flagged as *Unreviewed*. |
| **Payment status** | If payment collection is enabled for your account, the inspection fee has been recorded or marked as invoiced. |

<Warning>
  Do not mark an inspection complete if any findings are still unreviewed. Unreviewed findings—especially AI-suggested ones—may contain inaccurate information. Every finding in a completed report represents your professional assessment, so confirm each one before finalizing.
</Warning>

## How to Complete an Inspection and Generate the Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inspection">
    From your **Dashboard**, tap the inspection you are ready to finalize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the pre-completion checklist">
    Tap the **Checklist** icon at the top of the inspection overview. Review each item and resolve any that are flagged in red before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Mark as Complete'">
    Once all checklist items are green, tap the **Mark as Complete** button at the bottom of the inspection overview screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm completion">
    A confirmation dialog appears summarizing the number of areas, findings, and photos in the inspection. Tap **Complete Inspection** to confirm. InspectIQ begins generating your report immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for report generation">
    Report generation typically takes 10–30 seconds. A progress bar appears on the inspection overview screen. You'll receive an in-app notification when the report is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the report">
    Tap **Preview Report** to open a full-screen preview of the finished document. Scroll through all pages to verify formatting, photos, findings, and your branding look exactly as expected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver the report">
    When you're satisfied with the preview, tap **Send to Client** to email the report directly from InspectIQ, or tap **Download PDF** to save a copy and send it through your preferred channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Previewing the Report Before Sending

The report preview is a live render of the final document—what you see is exactly what your client receives. While in preview mode you can:

- **Scroll through all pages** to check every section.
- **Tap any finding** to jump back to the finding editor if you spot a typo or want to adjust wording. Your change re-renders automatically.
- **Check your logo and contact info** in the header and footer. Update these in **Settings → Branding** if anything looks off.
- **Share a preview link** with a colleague for a second opinion before sending to the client.

## Re-opening an Inspection

If you need to add or change a finding after marking the inspection complete, you can re-open it at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the completed inspection">
    Find the inspection on your **Dashboard** (it appears under the *Completed* tab) and tap to open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Re-open Inspection'">
    Tap the **⋯ More** menu at the top-right and select **Re-open Inspection**. The status changes back to *In Progress*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Add, edit, or remove findings as needed, then run through the pre-completion checklist again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark complete and regenerate the report">
    Follow the completion steps above. InspectIQ generates a new version of the report. If you previously sent the report to a client, notify them that a revised version is available and re-send using **Send to Client**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
