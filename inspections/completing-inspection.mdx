Before preparing your report, review the inspection workspace to make sure the information you've collected is accurate and complete.

## Review the Inspection Workspace

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you're completing.
3. Review the listed areas and rooms, their status, and the inspection progress indicators.

<img
  src="/images/01-inspection-workspace-1.jpg"
  alt="01 Inspection Workspace 1"
  title="01 Inspection Workspace 1"
/>

## Finish Each Applicable Area or Room

1. Select **Edit / Inspect** for the area or room.
2. Review the **Area / Room Overview** for accuracy.
3. Check the documented findings and any supporting photos or voice notes.
4. Make corrections where needed and use **Mark Area Complete** when the area is ready, if that action is available in your current view.

<img
  src="/images/02-area-overview.jpg"
  alt="02 Area Overview"
  title="02 Area Overview"
/>

<Note>
  Completing an area is not the same as finalizing an inspection report. You can return to the inspection workspace to continue reviewing other areas.
</Note>

## Review and Approve Findings

For each relevant finding, confirm its **title, severity, inspection category, observation, recommendation, and supporting evidence**. Edit anything that doesn't accurately reflect what you inspected.

If a finding still needs approval, open it, review the content, and select **Approve Finding**.

<img
  src="/images/03-review-finding.jpg"
  alt="03 Review Finding"
  title="03 Review Finding"
/>

<Warning>
  AI-generated content is a draft. The inspector remains responsible for verifying every observation and recommendation before approval.
</Warning>

For more on this step, see [AI Findings](/inspections/ai-findings).

## Check Inspection Progress

Return to the inspection workspace and review any incomplete areas or outstanding findings. Open the relevant area if you need to add or correct information.

<img
  src="/images/04-inspection-progress.jpg"
  alt="04 Inspection Progress"
  title="04 Inspection Progress"
/>

## Before You Move to Reports

Confirm that:

- All applicable areas and rooms have been reviewed.
- Overviews and relevant findings accurately describe the inspected conditions.
- Findings requiring approval have been reviewed and approved.
- Photos and voice notes support the documented observations.
- No important inspection information is missing.

<Tip>
  You don't need to create a finding for every area. Focus on communicating relevant observed conditions accurately.
</Tip>

When your inspection content is ready, continue to [Reports Overview](/reports/reports-overview) to prepare and review your reports.
