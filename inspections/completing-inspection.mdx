Once you've finished inspecting the property, InspectIQ helps you review your documented observations and prepare the inspection for reporting.

Before generating your reports, make sure your inspection areas, overviews, photos, and findings accurately reflect the conditions observed.

## Review Your Inspection

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you're working on.
3. Review the areas and rooms listed in the inspection workspace.

Check that you've addressed all applicable areas and documented any relevant concerns.

## Complete Each Area or Room

As you finish inspecting an area, you can mark it complete.

1. Open the area or room using **Edit / Inspect**.
2. Review the **Area / Room Overview**.
3. Check the findings documented in that area.
4. Make any necessary corrections.
5. Select **Mark Area Complete**.

This helps you track which parts of the inspection you've finished.

<Note>
  Marking an individual area complete is different from finalizing the overall inspection report.
</Note>

## Review Area / Room Overviews

Before finishing an area, review its overview to ensure it accurately describes the inspected conditions.

Check that:

- The description reflects your observations.
- Any AI-generated wording has been reviewed.
- Supporting photos are appropriate.
- The overview doesn't contain unsupported assumptions.

If changes are needed, use the available editing or regeneration controls.

## Review Inspection Findings

Review the findings you've created throughout the inspection.

For each finding, confirm that:

- The title accurately identifies the condition.
- The selected severity is appropriate.
- The inspection category is correct.
- The observation matches what you inspected.
- The recommendation is clear and reasonable.
- Supporting photos are relevant.
- Any maintenance recommendations or disclaimers are appropriate.

### Approve Outstanding Findings

If a finding hasn't been approved:

1. Open the finding.
2. Review the AI-generated or manually entered content.
3. Make any necessary changes.
4. Select **Approve Finding**.

<Warning>
  Always verify AI-generated findings before approving them. The inspector remains responsible for the accuracy of the inspection report.
</Warning>

## Check Your Inspection Progress

Return to the main inspection page to review the status of your areas and rooms.

Use the available status indicators to identify areas that may still require attention.

If you discover missing information, reopen the applicable area or room and update its content.

<Tip>
  Review your inspection area by area rather than relying on memory at the end of the day. This helps reduce the chance of overlooking observations.
</Tip>

## Prepare for Report Generation

Once you've reviewed your inspection content, you're ready to prepare your reports.

InspectIQ's Report Builder brings together your inspection information and provides tools to prepare the final documents.

The Report Builder includes:

- Report Title
- Executive Summary
- Safety Concerns
- Major Concerns
- Maintenance Recommendations

You can review and edit the report content before finalizing it.

## Generate Your Report Summary

Within the Report Builder, select **Generate AI Summary** to prepare suggested summary content using your documented inspection findings.

Review the generated text carefully and make any necessary adjustments.

<Note>
  Report summaries are AI-assisted and should be verified before the report is finalized.
</Note>

## Preview and Finalize Your Reports

The Report Builder provides controls for the final reporting process, including:

- **Save Report** — Save your report content.
- **Preview Report** — Review the report before finalizing it.
- **Finalize Report** — Finalize the report using the available workflow.
- **Download Summary** — Download the Summary Report.
- **Download Full** — Download the Full Inspection Report.

Before finalizing, review both report documents to ensure the information is accurate and complete.

<Tip>
  Preview your reports before finalization. Pay particular attention to the Executive Summary, major concerns, safety findings, photographs, and recommendations.
</Tip>

## Final Inspection Checklist

Before finalizing your report, confirm that:

- All applicable areas and rooms have been inspected.
- Completed areas have been marked accordingly.
- Area / Room Overviews have been reviewed.
- Relevant findings have been documented and approved.
- Photos and supporting information are accurate.
- The report summary has been reviewed.
- The generated reports accurately reflect the inspection.

Once everything has been reviewed, you can proceed with finalizing your reports.

<Note>
  Completing inspection areas and finalizing reports are separate steps. Use the Report Builder to review and finalize the documents prepared from your inspection.
</Note>
