Before you complete an inspection, InspectIQ gives you a dedicated review screen where you can fine-tune every finding—whether it came from the AI assistant or you wrote it yourself. Here you can update titles, descriptions, severity levels, and photos; reorder or move findings between areas; add recommendations; and bulk-mark items as reviewed so nothing slips through the cracks.

## Accessing the Findings Review Screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the inspection">
    From your **Dashboard**, tap the inspection you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Review Findings'">
    On the inspection overview screen, tap the **Review Findings** button. This opens the findings review screen, which lists every finding across all areas in one scrollable view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter or search if needed">
    Use the **Filter** menu at the top to narrow the list by area, severity, or review status. Tap the search icon to find a specific finding by keyword.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review your findings on-site before you leave the property. If you spot a gap—a missing photo, an unclear description, or an area you haven't visited yet—you can fix it immediately while the details are fresh and the property is still accessible.
</Tip>

## Editing a Finding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap the finding you want to edit">
    In the findings review screen, tap any finding to open its detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the title">
    Tap the **Title** field and type your changes. Keep titles concise and descriptive—clients read these first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the description">
    Tap the **Description** field to expand the text editor. Write a clear explanation of what you observed, where it is located, and why it matters. Use plain language your client will understand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust the severity">
    Tap the **Severity** selector and choose the appropriate level:

    - **Informational** — items to note for awareness, no immediate action needed
    - **Low** — minor issues that should be monitored or addressed over time
    - **Moderate** — issues that warrant timely repair or further evaluation
    - **High** — significant defects requiring prompt attention
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add, replace, or remove photos">
    Scroll to the **Photos** section of the finding. Tap **Add Photo** to attach another image, tap an existing photo to replace it, or swipe left on a photo thumbnail and tap **Remove** to delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Tap **Save** in the top-right corner. Your edits are saved immediately and reflected in the findings list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reordering Findings Within an Area

The order of findings in a given area is the order they appear in the client report. You can rearrange them at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to area view">
    In the findings review screen, tap **Group by Area** in the Filter menu to organize findings by area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter reorder mode">
    Tap the **Edit Order** button at the top-right of the area section you want to reorganize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag findings into the right order">
    Press and hold the drag handle (the three horizontal lines) on the left side of a finding, then drag it up or down to your preferred position.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Done'">
    Tap **Done** to save the new order. The updated sequence is immediately reflected in the report preview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Moving a Finding to a Different Area

If a finding was logged under the wrong area, you can move it without losing any of its details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the finding">
    Tap the finding you want to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Move to Area'">
    Scroll to the bottom of the finding detail view and tap **Move to Area**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the destination area">
    Choose the correct area from the list of areas in this inspection. The finding moves instantly and disappears from its original area.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk-Reviewing Multiple Findings

When you have a long list of findings to sign off on, bulk review saves you time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap 'Select' in the findings review screen">
    Tap the **Select** button in the top-right corner of the findings review screen. Checkboxes appear next to each finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the findings to mark as reviewed">
    Tap each finding you want to mark, or tap **Select All** at the top to select every finding in the current filtered view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Mark as Reviewed'">
    Tap the **Mark as Reviewed** button in the toolbar at the bottom of the screen. All selected findings are updated at once, and a green checkmark appears on each one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Adding a Recommendation or Action Item

Every finding can include one or more recommendations—specific actions you want your client to take.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the finding">
    Tap the finding you want to update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to 'Recommendations'">
    Scroll down to the **Recommendations** section within the finding detail view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Add Recommendation'">
    Tap **Add Recommendation** and type your action item (e.g., "Have a licensed electrician evaluate and repair the double-tapped breaker").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a priority (optional)">
    Toggle the **High Priority** switch if this is an action the client should take immediately. High-priority recommendations are highlighted in the client report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the finding">
    Tap **Save**. The recommendation is attached to the finding and will appear in the report directly beneath the finding description.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The report preview shows exactly how your findings will appear to the client—formatting, photos, severity badges, and recommendations included. Tap **Preview Report** at any time from the inspection overview screen to check your work before you finalize.
</Note>
