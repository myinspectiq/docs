InspectIQ makes documenting inspection observations easier using photos and voice notes.

Whether you're working from a desktop computer, mobile browser, or the InspectIQ phone app, you can capture supporting information and use it to prepare AI-assisted inspection findings.

## Open an Inspection Area

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you're working on.
3. Find the appropriate area or room.
4. Select **Edit / Inspect**.
5. Locate the **Findings** section.

Select **Capture Finding** to begin documenting an observation.

## Adding Inspection Photos

Photos help document property conditions and provide visual context for inspection findings.

### On Desktop

If you're using InspectIQ on a desktop computer:

1. Open **Capture Finding**.
2. Select **Upload Photo**.
3. Choose an image from your computer.
4. Repeat as needed to add additional photos.

### On Mobile or the Phone App

When using InspectIQ on your phone:

1. Open the inspection and select the appropriate area or room.
2. Select **Capture Finding**.
3. Tap **Capture Photo**.
4. Your device's camera will open.
5. Take a photo and confirm it using your device's camera controls.

You can also upload existing photos from your device using the available upload option.

<Note>
  The **Capture Photo** button is available in the mobile version and phone app. On desktop, you can upload existing photos from your computer.
</Note>

## Editing Inspection Photos

InspectIQ includes photo editing tools to help highlight important details.

After adding a photo, use the available editing controls to make adjustments.

Depending on the available tools, you can:

- Crop the image.
- Add arrows to highlight specific conditions.
- Adjust the image to focus attention on relevant details.

<Tip>
  Use arrows or annotations to identify specific defects without obscuring the original condition being documented.
</Tip>

## Recording Voice Notes

Voice notes allow you to describe inspection observations without typing.

This is particularly useful when inspecting a property and moving between areas.

### Record a Voice Note

1. Open **Capture Finding** within the applicable area or room.
2. Select **Record Voice Note**.
3. Describe your observation clearly.
4. Stop the recording when finished.

### Add Another Voice Note

If you need to provide additional context:

1. Select **Add Another Voice Note**.
2. Record the additional information.
3. Repeat as needed.

Multiple voice notes can provide additional context for the same finding.

## What Should You Include in a Voice Note?

For more accurate AI-generated findings, try to describe:

- **Location:** Where the condition was observed.
- **Observation:** What you saw during the inspection.
- **Concern:** Why the condition may require attention.
- **Recommendation:** Any action or further evaluation you believe is appropriate.

For example:

"Basement utility room. There is visible water staining along the lower wall near the foundation. Recommend further evaluation to determine the source of moisture."

<Tip>
  Speak naturally and describe what you actually observe. You don't need to dictate perfectly formatted report language—InspectIQ helps organize the information into a structured finding.
</Tip>

## Using Photos and Voice Notes Together

You can include both photos and voice notes when documenting a finding.

Photos provide visual evidence, while voice notes explain the observation and provide additional context.

Once you've added your information, select **Generate AI Finding** to create a suggested finding.

InspectIQ uses the supplied information to help prepare structured report content for your review.

## Review Your Captured Information

Before generating a finding, confirm that:

- Your photos show the condition clearly.
- Any annotations accurately identify the concern.
- Your voice notes describe the correct location and observation.
- You've included enough information to support a meaningful finding.

<Warning>
  AI-assisted findings depend on the information provided. Always review generated content for accuracy and ensure recommendations reflect the actual conditions observed.
</Warning>

## What Happens Next?

After capturing your photos and voice notes, you can generate an AI-assisted finding.

InspectIQ can prepare the finding's title, category, severity, observation, and recommendation for your review.

You remain in control of reviewing, editing, regenerating, and approving the final finding.
