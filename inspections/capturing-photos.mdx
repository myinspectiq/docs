title: "Capturing Photos & Voice Notes"
description: "Capture inspection photos, edit images, and record voice notes to document findings in InspectIQ."
icon: "camera"
---

Photos and voice notes help you document what you observe on site. Add either or both when capturing a finding, then use that evidence to generate an AI-assisted suggestion for your review.

## Open Capture Finding

1. Go to **Inspections** and open the inspection.
2. Select **Edit / Inspect** for the appropriate area or room.
3. Under **Findings**, select **Capture Finding**.

<img
  src="/images/01-capture-finding.jpg"
  alt="01 Capture Finding"
  title="01 Capture Finding"
/>

## Add Inspection Photos

### Desktop: Upload Photo

Select **Upload Photo**, choose an existing image from your computer, and add more photos as needed.

### Mobile browser and phone app: Capture Photo

On a phone, **Capture Photo** also appears. Tap it to open your device camera, take a photo, and confirm it. You can still use **Upload Photo** to select an existing image, including a drone or previously captured photo.

<img
  src="/images/Mobile-Capture-Finding_v2.png"
  alt="Mobile Capture Finding V2"
  title="Mobile Capture Finding V2"
/>

<Note>
  **Capture Photo** is available in the mobile version and phone app. Desktop uses **Upload Photo**.
</Note>

After adding a photo, it appears in the Photos section alongside your voice notes.

<img
  src="/images/03-uploaded-photo.jpg"

  title="03 Uploaded Photo"

## Edit a Photo

Use the available photo editing controls to highlight an important condition. The editor shown in the recording supports actions such as **Crop** and **Arrow**; finish by saving your changes.

<img
  src="/images/07-photo-editor.jpg"
  alt="07 Photo Editor"
  title="07 Photo Editor"
/>

<Tip>
  Use annotations sparingly so the relevant condition remains easy to see.
</Tip>

## Record Voice Notes

1. Select **Record Voice Note**.
2. Describe the observed condition in your own words.
3. Stop the recording when finished.
4. Use **Add Another Voice Note** if more detail is needed.

Your recordings are displayed in the Voice Notes section, where you can review the captured audio and its available transcript.

<img
  src="/images/04-voice-note-1.jpg"
  alt="04 Voice Note 1"

/>

<img
  src="/images/05-multiple-voice-notes.jpg"

  title="05 Multiple Voice Notes"
/>

<Tip>
  State **where** the condition is, **what** you observed, and any relevant concern or recommendation. You don't need to dictate report-ready wording.
</Tip>

## Review Your Evidence

Before selecting **Generate AI Finding**, make sure your photos show the condition clearly and your voice notes accurately describe it. You can combine photos and voice notes to provide more context for one finding.

<img
  src="/images/06-evidence-ready.jpg"
  alt="06 Evidence Ready"
  title="06 Evidence Ready"
/>

When the supporting information is ready, select **Generate AI Finding**. InspectIQ will prepare a suggestion for you to review and approve.

<Warning>
  You remain responsible for confirming that every generated observation and recommendation reflects the conditions you actually inspected.
</Warning>

For the next step, see [AI Findings](/inspections/ai-findings).
