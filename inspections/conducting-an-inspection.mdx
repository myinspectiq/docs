InspectIQ helps you work through each area or room, document what you observe, and turn photos and voice notes into AI-assisted findings for your review.

## Open an Inspection

1. Select **Inspections** from the main navigation.
2. Open the inspection you want to work on (or select **Start Inspection** or **Continue Inspection**, when shown).
3. Review the property details, inspection progress, and available areas and rooms.

![01 Inspection Workspace](/images/01-inspection-workspace.jpg "01 Inspection Workspace")

## Understanding the Inspection Workspace

From the inspection workspace, you can search for an area or room, filter by group, add an area or room, open an existing area, and add or change the **Report Cover Photo**.

![02 Area Room List](/images/02-area-room-list.jpg "02 Area Room List")

<Tip>
  Set up your preferred areas and rooms under **Business Settings → Inspection Setup** before creating new inspections.
</Tip>

## Navigate Areas & Rooms

Areas and rooms are organized into groups, such as **Exterior** and **Interior**.

1. Find the area or room you want to inspect.
2. Open it using **Edit / Inspect**, where shown.
3. Review its overview and findings section.

## Area / Room Overview

The **Area / Room Overview** provides general information about the selected area. Review any suggested content and use the available controls to **Approve Overview**, **Edit**, **Regenerate**, or **Re-record** as appropriate. The overview also supports photo uploads.

![03 Area Overview](/images/03-area-overview.jpg "03 Area Overview")

<Note>
  An area overview provides general context. Use **Capture Finding** to document a specific condition as an individual finding. Always verify suggested content before approval.
</Note>

## Document Your Observations

Within an area or room, select **Capture Finding** to add the supporting evidence for an observation.

![04 Capture Finding](/images/04-capture-finding.jpg "04 Capture Finding")

### Photos

On **desktop**, choose **Upload Photo** to select an existing image. On the **mobile version and phone app**, **Capture Photo** is also available and opens your device camera; you can still upload existing images.

### Voice Notes

Select **Record Voice Note** to describe what you observe. Use **Add Another Voice Note** if you need a separate recording. Photos and voice notes can be included in the same finding.

![05 Finding Evidence](/images/05-finding-evidence.jpg "05 Finding Evidence")

<Tip>
  Describe the condition, its location, and why it matters. Specific observations help InspectIQ generate a more useful draft.
</Tip>

## Generate AI Findings

When you've added your photos and/or voice notes, select **Generate AI Finding**. InspectIQ prepares a suggested finding based on the evidence provided.

The suggestion can include a **title**, **severity**, **inspection category**, **observation**, **recommendation**, **maintenance recommendation**, and **disclaimer note**.

## Review and Approve Findings

Review the suggested finding and edit the available fields to match your actual observations. You can then select **Approve Finding**, **Regenerate AI** for a new draft, or **Delete** to remove the suggestion.

![06 Ai Suggested Finding](/images/06-ai-suggested-finding.jpg "06 Ai Suggested Finding")

<Warning>
  AI-generated text is a suggestion, not an inspection decision. Confirm the accuracy, severity, and recommendations before approving a finding.
</Warning>

## Continue Through Your Inspection

Return to the area list and repeat the process for other applicable areas and rooms. You can revisit areas to review their overviews, add evidence, and update findings. The **Inspections** page also shows your inspections by status, with **Start Inspection** and **Continue Inspection** actions where applicable.

![07 Inspection Progress](/images/07-inspection-progress.jpg "07 Inspection Progress")

## Before Completing Your Inspection

Check that applicable areas have been addressed, important conditions are documented, supporting evidence is included where needed, and suggested findings have been reviewed and approved. You can then proceed to inspection completion and report preparation.

<Tip>
  You don't need an individual finding for every area or room. Document relevant conditions that should be communicated to your client.
</Tip>
