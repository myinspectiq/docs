InspectIQ is designed to keep you moving efficiently on-site. Whether you're inspecting a single-family home or a large commercial property, the app walks you through a consistent workflow — helping you capture photos, record findings, and generate a polished report without missing a beat.

## The Inspection Workflow

Here's how a typical inspection flows inside InspectIQ, from the moment you arrive to the moment you wrap up:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Work Order">
    From the **Home** screen, tap the work order for today's inspection. Review the property details, client information, and any notes added during scheduling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Inspection">
    Tap **Start Inspection** to activate the inspection session. InspectIQ loads your template and sets up all the areas and sections for the property type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate Areas">
    Move through each area of the property — such as Roof, Foundation, Kitchen, or Electrical Panel — using the area list. Tap any area to open it and begin documenting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture Photos">
    Use the in-app camera to photograph each area and any items you want to document. Add annotations to highlight specific concerns directly on the photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record Findings">
    Add findings for defects, maintenance items, and observations. Assign a severity level, write a description, and attach photos to each finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use AI-Generated Findings">
    Let InspectIQ's AI analyze your photos and suggest findings automatically. Review the suggestions, edit as needed, and accept what's accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Edit">
    When you've covered every area, do a final pass through your findings. Check descriptions, confirm severity ratings, and make sure every item is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Inspection">
    Tap **Complete Inspection** to finalize your session. InspectIQ compiles everything into your report, ready to review and deliver to your client.
  </Step>
</Steps>
