InspectIQ simplifies the Inspection Agreement process by allowing inspectors to generate agreements, send them to clients for electronic signature, and track their completion directly from a Work Order.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the Inspection Agreement">
    Open the appropriate **Work Order** and select **Generate Agreement**.

    InspectIQ generates the agreement using the information associated with the Work Order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Send the Agreement">
    Select **Preview Agreement** to review the document before sending it.

    Once you're satisfied, select **Send Agreement**.

    InspectIQ emails the client a secure link to review and electronically sign the agreement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Client Reviews and Signs">
    Your client receives an email containing a link to **Review & Sign Agreement**.

    From there, they can:

    - Review the Inspection Agreement.
    - Accept the electronic signing terms.
    - Add their electronic signature.
    - Complete the signing process.

    Once completed, the agreement is ready for the inspector's signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Inspector Signature">
    Return to the Work Order in InspectIQ.

    Review the client's completed agreement and add your electronic signature to finalize the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Agreement Completion">
    Once both parties have signed, the agreement status updates to **Completed (Signed)**.

    The Work Order displays **Inspection Ready**, allowing you to proceed with the inspection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always review the Inspection Agreement before sending it to ensure the information is accurate and complete.
</Tip>
